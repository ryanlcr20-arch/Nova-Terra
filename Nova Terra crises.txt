Nova Terran Wars


Rapusian Overseas War (1947-?)
Rapusia, even after the Great War, had massive colonies, especially in Libra
As a consequence, Rapusia had to deal with persistent, low-level insurgency, which sometimes flared up into even larger uprisings like in Nyassaland ‘55
These uprisings were frequently suppressed with brutality, though by the 1960s there was more oversight from civilian authorities in Brandenstadt 
Often, though, they signed off on the same brutal practices that the Wehrmacht did when they were unsupervised…
Grease War (1948-1949): Kekistan vs. Cavalistan (Cavalistani victory)
First proxy war of the Cold War (Rapusia vs. Canausbel)
Kekistan invaded Cavalistan for oil, justified it with something else (border dispute)
Cavalistan pushed back, taking some of southwest Kekistan in the process
National pride increase in Cavalistan (already high off victory in the Great War), instability in Kekistan that led to military coup
One of the last truly conventional wars
Anglo-Twittertopian War (1948-1959; kinetic phase 1958-1959): Twittertopia vs. Commonwealth (Commonwealth victory)
Persistent low-level naval shenanigans and denounciations by Twittertopia (seeking to annex the Commonwealth’s Hydran Sea colonies for their valuable resources), skirmishes galore (1948-58)
In the final phase of the conflict (1958-59), Twittertopia directly attacked the territories they claimed, but was repulsed and placed under a blockade by the Commonwealth
The blockade caused a humanitarian crisis, which the CoN partially succeeded in mitigating by sending aid and later mediating a ceasefire 
The Commonwealth won, but had a taint on its international reputation due to the humanitarian crisis caused, which in part led to the Twittertopian Civil War beginning later that year
Trarori-Jesusland War (1950-1958): Jesusland vs. Traroria (Trarorian victory)
Started over a territorial dispute over the lands surrounding Lake Traror (controlled and claimed by Traroria)
Porte Alegreya, Traroria’s capital, is located on the north coast of the lake, but has a sizeable Jesuslander population
Jesusland invaded, which led to a protracted conflict reminiscent of the early Great War in trench warfare, but in rough terrain due to their relative military parity
Jesusland was repulsed, and signed a peace treaty renouncing its claims on Trarorian territory, but became closer to Rapusia & the Taunfurt Pact as a result
Skirmishes continue between them
Nyassaland Uprising (1955-1956)
A colonial uprising in Rapusian Nyassaland
Brutally suppressed using such lovely tactics as carpet-bombing and summary execution of alleged rebels
International condemnation, but at the same time this was a Great Power which promoted sovereignty (and people ended up backing Rapusia anyway), so nothing happened in the meantime
Gyattisland Bush War (1958-1976): Cartegenia & Gyattisland vs. Rapusia & Mianmaur People’s Liberation Army (Rapusian victory through pro-Rapusian ceasefire)
Fought over Gyattisland, a Cartegenian client state used as a buffer against the Rapusian colonies and a container for restive ethnicities (including the Mianmau)
Guerilla warfare was present along with conventional warfare between Cartegenian-supported forces and Rapusia-supported forces
Some people in the Rapusian colonies seized the opportunity to flee to Cartegenia, while others tried to flee to Central Land (with mixed results)
Ended with a CoN-mediated ceasefire that made Rapusia guarantee Gyattisland’s independence while Gyattisland remained ostensibly sovereign, but still quite unstable and essentially one bad sneeze away from collapsing into a patchwork of ethnic states
Twittertopian Civil War (1959-?): Twittertopia vs. National Salvation Government vs. other factions

NSG uses guerilla warfare; insurgency continues
Commonwealth & Reddit Island support the NSG (the former because it’s pro-stability; the other because it’s anti-Twittertopia)
Twittertopian government (more specifically, the Peace Force) establishes corporate freeports and a drug industry to fuel the war effort 
Some of the natives seize the opportunity to rise up and proclaim the Republic of San Miguel (named after the former name of Twitter City, the capital), headquartered in the resort town of Montaklar
Community of Nations Peacekeepers engaged in humanitarian work
Refugee crisis affects Maralgorange, Flipadossa, Kirbistan and Auxur
Some allege that the Great Syndicate is an extension of Maralgorange’s crime networks, even if Maralgorange is officially neutral in the conflict
Second Ataraxi-Dupelander War (1961-1968): Ataraxia vs. Dupeland (Status quo bellum ante)
Fought over water rights in the region
Dupeland is fighting for survival (but is a theocratic absolute monarchy) and Ataraxia is a democracy (a corrupt, vaguely religious-socialist one with a weaker claim)
Proxy war between Canausbel (Ataraxia) and Rapusia (Dupeland)
War fought using Great War-era technology, primarily in the mountains (both sides attempted to use guerilla warfare on each other; failing miserably and causing thousands of casualties)
Ended in a stalemate, mediated by the UTP, and even then minor skirmishes continue between them
Ishanian Crisis (1961-1974)
Started after the Ishanian Army fired upon striking workers with artillery
Escalated into a full-blown rebellion led principally by the pro-democracy (Tridemist) Society of Ishanian Nationalists, organised into the Blue Battalions
In some Nationalist-controlled areas, a proto-state emerged known as the Ishanian Republic
Many warlords tried to remain neutral, but frequently ended up crushed by the Imperials or overthrown from the bottom by the Nationalists
Although the Nationalists lost in the end, Ishanis ended up adopting universal male suffrage for the Legislative Yuan in 1979, vindicating the Nationalists retroactively
All in all, about 12 million Ishanians died because of the insurgency and its consequences 
To boot, in the first (relatively) free elections in Ishanian history, in 1979, a new organisation ended up winning in a landslide. What were they named? THE SOCIETY OF ISHANIAN NATIONALISTS. (A revival of the original Society rather than the exact same party, technically, but still)
To this day, people think that if women were allowed to vote, it would be very possible that the Nationalists would win an even more lopsided landslide
Syudd War (1964-1977): People’s Democratic Liberation Front of Syudd vs. Commonwealth, Syudd (PDLF of Syudd victory)
Syudd was historically a Commonwealth protectorate since the 1880s
Syudd gained independence in 1962
Nationalist-socialist insurgency!
International criticism of the Commonwealth’s intervention 
The Syuddese clearly just wanted the Commonwealth to leave already
After a long, asymmetric war, the Commonwealth withdrew in 1976, fueling a sense of bitterness in the Commonwealth
War crimes everywhere (though particularly common amongst the rebels)
Some spillover into neighbouring Rapusian colonies, which used the inspiration of the rebels to rise in open revolt against their colonial overlords
Androsia War/Two-Day War (1968): Rapusia, Silent Devils vs. Androsia (Rapusian victory)
Rapusia sought to establish a land corridor to Qurkia via this territory (connecting the Libran colonies to another Taunfurt Pact member via Androsia and friendly Jesusland) and tie up loose ends regarding Androsia’s disloyal alignment with Rapusia in the 1950s
Androsian military was ill-prepared, and there was severe unrest in the country from the underclass of beta males (largely farmers, workers or soldiers) and women that consumed most of the military’s attention
Rapusia successfully curried favour for an intervention from the international community (and a mercenary company)
In the aftermath of a swift campaign (fueled by overextension and encirclement of Androsian forces by internal unrest), a Rapusian puppet state was established (later named Elefthera in 1972 after popular referendum)
Sharisam War (1971-1973)
Sharisam announced its intentions to terminate its observer status in the International as the population and leadership continued to drift towards the Taunfurt Pact and Dai-Koku in 1970
There was already an oppressed Timernondese minority in Sharisam, which Timernond used as an excuse to invade Sharisam
By the end of this short, but bloody war, upwards to 300,000 had died, and Sharisam was in turmoil, leading to a socialist revolution anyway
Rapusian invasion of Syudd (1980)
The PDLF had somehow managed to kill 30% of Syudd’s population through their own insane policies
Rapusia invaded after Syudd attacked Rapusian and Dupelander border outposts
The war itself only lasted three weeks before the capital of Syudd, Valmari, fell to Rapusia
The Republic of Syudd was established as a Rapusian puppet state under Pact-Commission Syudd; and had to deal with mopping up the PDLF remnants and rebuilding
This is gonna stink, isn’t it?
Foreign interventions (and other incidents) in Nova Terra
Scramble for Terra Antipolaris (1923-)
Began after the end of the first phase of the Great War, but went into high gear after the end of the Great War
Primarily due to Terra Antipolaris’ (presumed) untapped resources, and the prestige of having people on the last southern frontier of Nova Terra
The Commonwealth, Diplomatians, Western Quaristanicaval, the UTP, Rapusia, Canausbel, Dai-Koku and Pangarap all have territories here, often overlapping (in the case of the Commonwealth and Western Quaristanicaval, it resulted in a minor territorial dispute resolved peacefully)
Rapusian Crisis (1956)
Caused by the rising cost of living, crumbling infrastructure, austerity and corruption
Led by the Independent Social-Democratic Labour Party (while still banned) and the SDPR
Primarily marked by strikes, walkouts (like at the Wolecław arms works), sit-ins, protests and riots, and in one case a naval mutiny
Some radicals seized the opportunity to occupy public spaces and government buildings, leading to the characterization of said radicals as putschists
Suppressed by the military, violently, but in the end did lead to the parliamentary accountability of the Chancellor
Stormy Sea Crisis (1956)
The Commonwealth had incurred a massive national debt from its reconstruction efforts, much of it from North Contanan investments
The Grand Pensionary, Jan Sterren, decided that enough was enough with this debt, and so threatened the Commonwealth to force repayment
This was a bad idea, because the Commonwealth mobilized its own forces in response, leading to a tense stand-off, with the Commonwealth threatening to nuke North Contana if it went through with its threats
North Contana, notably, did not possess nuclear weapons at this time
The UTP saved the day by threatening an embargo of North Contanan manganese if they didn’t back off
Shortly thereafter, the Sterren government faced a constructive vote of no confidence, and failed, leading to the appointment of a new Grand Pensionary who decided to stand down North Contana’s forces, ending the crisis
The Stadshallare profusely apologized for the incident, promising that such a thing would never happen again 
Rapusia didn’t do anything here because it was preoccupied with its own problems (the Rapusian Crisis of 1956), and everyone’s attention was more focused on the possible collapse of a superpower
First Quaristanicaval Crisis (1959)
Pangarap threatened war with Western Quaristanicaval
The Community of Nations in response slapped Pangarap down with even harsher sanctions (during a famine), and all of the Great Powers said “no”
Pangarap backed down, but clearly only at the behest of the Great Powers the leadership clearly resented
Assassination of Franco Prat (1960)
Franco Prat was the newly elected Duke of Salmana and President of the Trade Confederacy (a strategically situated nation in the southern part of Central Hydra, home to the Salmana Canal, a critical maritime thoroughfare between the Central Ocean and the Great Ocean), who was a populist authoritarian (who frequently kidnapped political opponents and even used violence against protestors at one point) who advocated for more firm Salmanese neutrality and national sovereignty over the Salmana Canal Zone (rather than joint Tankian-Salmanese sovereignty in a condominium)
He was assassinated in 1960 at a rally, officially by a disgruntled intern; but many outside the FNA believe it to be an OSIS operation, and that the intern was used by OSIS to get rid of a possible enemy
Highlands Incident (1960)
A Rapusian spy plane was shot down over the Scotian Highlands
For three weeks, there were tense negotiations over the return of that pilot
The pilot was returned in the end, and he wrote a memoir that indicted Rapusia’s handling of the situation
First Skiyian Crisis (1961)
In part caused by the coup d’état in Skiyia earlier that year
Escalated to almost another Great War when a stray weather balloon flew over Rapusian airspace during a FNA military exercise about a response to a hypothetical conventional war with the Reichspakt
The poor weather balloon was shot down and was determined to be such, but not before Rapusia mobilized forces, and Skiyia mobilized forces in response
Cooler heads prevailed, though, and nuclear war was averted 
Central Lander refugee crisis (1962)
An unusually large number of people fled from Gyattisland across the sea to Central Land that year (probably because of the Bush War), often in heavily overcrowded ships
Massive diplomatic pressure on Central Land to let them enter
In the end, about 60% of the refugees were turned back by the Central Lander Navy, and upwards to 17,000 died 
Koryos Missile Crisis (1966)
Koryos is a large Canausbelite island off its Annular coast, a demilitarized zone ever since Canausbel regained it from Rapusia in 1947 (a major sore spot for Canausbel)
Canausbel violated the provision by stationing IRBMs on it
In response, Rapusia stationed ICBMs as close as they could to the Canausbelite border, and Canausbel followed suit
However, negotiations between Rapusia and Canausbel managed to prevent crisis
The Protests of 1968 (1968)
Many things were protested this year, especially in the UTP, Rapusia and Commonwealth (and sometimes led to riots)
The Commonwealth’s involvement in Syudd, Rapusia’s colonial empire, the events in Ishanis and the emerging gay acceptance movements (people accepted that gay people existed, they were just often seen as weird, and police in Rapusia did raid a major gay bar in Brandenstadt earlier that very year) were all major topics
This was about when the counterculture (environmentalist, somewhat leftist, still nationalist but more likely to accept that all nations are equal, certainly anti-imperialist)  became visible for the first time in FNA and Taunfurt Pact countries, via music festivals and festival festivals in general, where “normal” people and “beatniks” hung around learning from each other for the first time
The gay acceptance movement (or Free Love movement) ended up being the only major one that got its goals accomplished in the reasonably near future
Second Kekistani Revolution (1968)
Kekistan was becoming increasingly unstable due to declining oil prices and an economy seemingly entirely dependent on that oil
Conservative socialism was becoming a popular political current
Socialist elements within the military ended up launching a military coup, ousting Big Man Tyrone and aligning themselves with the International
Second Quaristanicaval Crisis (1973)
Pangarap once again threatened war against Western Quaristanicaval
Western Quaristanicaval decided that it wasn’t going to let the international community do the mediation for them, and mobilized its military and threatened to get the Great Powers on its side
Standoff…
International mediation happened anyway
Second Skiyian Crisis (1974)
The causes of this one were more vague (with one possible explanation being a Skiyian soldier kicking a pebble across the Rapusian border), but the world came quite close to another Great War; only stopped by the actions of one Tankian submarine captain, who decided not to fire ICBMs when ordered to by command.
17th of May Incident (1979)  -> Time of Troubles (1979-1995)
Increasing political violence in the country caused by increasing civil resistance to the colonial system in place since the 17th century (including disillusioned veterans, youth and of course the colonial populations themselves)
Military influence grew stronger over the course of the Gyattislander Bush War
When the protests reached fever pitch, the Emergency Management Council (a junta of various generals opposed to the system, or just opposed to instability) overthrow the civilian government of the National Party (right-wing, pro-Ustayaris System, militarist, dominant political party since the 1900s)
Under the EMC, many of the racial laws that existed were repealed, and the Bantustans were abolished
However, opposition figures and journalists were persecuted, and figures from the previous civilian government (including the former Prime Minister, Osman Reis, a National Party hardliner) were executed soon after the coup
Civilian government was restored in 1982, but this was criticized for it being a military dictatorship giving up power willingly, with the centre-left Republican Union winning the first elections after the regime
The last racial laws were rescinded in 1995 after a prolonged period of political instability (caused by a protracted loyalist insurgency), formally ending the Ustayaris System and the inequality of the colonial population
Third Skiyian Crisis (1982)
The Reichspakt did one of its greatest military exercises in its history, Militarubung 82
The exercise was such a realistic drill of what the Reichspakt would do in the case of a conventional war (based on the UTP’s intelligence) that war almost broke out, again
Several firm clarifications from Rapusia made the FNA calm down due to public negativity over “a rerun of the last crisis”
