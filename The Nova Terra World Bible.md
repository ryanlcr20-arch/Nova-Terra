# Nova Terra: A Reference Document 

“New World, Old Problems”

## ABSTRACT

This Reference Document is intended to allow the reader to gain a comprehensive understanding of the setting of Nova Terra, and thus participate in discussions surrounding the setting. Together with the Book (the physical notebook that contains many of the Country Information Cards), it has the final word as to whether a certain thing forms part of the “canon” or not. This should ideally be used like an encyclopedia (in other words, not read all at once), though one can use this entire document to get up to speed if they have missed many developments regarding Nova Terra’s lore. It is highly recommended, if you are forgetful, to take notes on things that you are likely to forget.

## THE WORLD

Nova Terra (HD 85512b) is an Earthlike habitable exoplanet orbiting HD 85512 (“Cirrhos”), a K-type star ~36 light-years from our world. It is accompanied by a singular moon (simply the Moon to its inhabitants), which has partially disintegrated into a ring system due to an impact from a rogue body estimated to have taken place 100 million years ago (causing a mass extinction), most prominent in equatorial skies and during the night, though in rare circumstances you can see them during the day. As a result, meteor showers are a relatively common event, and are prominent cultural events when they occur. It is also located slightly closer towards the outer edge of the habitable zone, and possesses comparable gravity to our home, and 70% of it is covered in water. It was colonised ~2,300 years before the setting's present day of 1963 NE, only for an unspecified catastrophe to sever contact with Earth; leaving most knowledge of it now lost, mythologised, or absorbed into religion. Its geography broadly resembles our Earth's but with a different tectonic history (and plenty of minor differences once you zoom in). Defined by a long, surprisingly detailed history that culminates in the modern world of Nova Terra and the ongoing Cold War between the Free Nations' Alliance, the Taunfurt Pact, and the Socialist International (or the Third International, or simply the International). Life goes on, though, for its ~3.5 billion people, even under the looming spectre of nuclear annihilation.

Even though this world isn’t Earth, **it sure does feel an awful lot like it.**

**Continents:** North Hydra (N. America), South Hydra (S. America), Columba (Europe), Libra (Africa), Antares (Asia), Cetus (Oceania), Terra Antipolaris (Antarctica).  
**Oceans and seas:** Central (Atlantic), Great (Pacific), Boreal (Arctic), Notian (Southern), Timernondese (Indian), Hydran (Caribbean), Annulus (Mediterranean), Stormy (North), Ataraxian (Red), Contanan (Baltic), Oculus (Black).

**The Living Tank** (*Loricatorum chariotus*): A species of arthropod-adjacent megafaunal apex predator that convergently evolved an almost exactly tank-like form over 500 million years, with layered chitin-biomineral exoskeleton, continuous organic treads, dorsal turret firing biomineral slugs, prehensile mandibles and pedipalps and haemo-hydraulic movement. Intelligent but not sapient, possessing pattern recognition (including some degree of linguistic response), spatial reasoning, emotional bonds and individual quirks (chirping, purring, wandering off when overheated, etc.). They have a typical attention span of ~10–15 minutes. Semi-aquatic and water-dependent due to their evolutionary history. Domesticated in many societies and legally protected in most countries, while used as pack animals, pets and warbeasts. A custodian is responsible for the well-being of a Living Tank under their care and can be a person or a juridical person (like the military); a handler is the one who directly cares for it (often the legal custodian in civilian cases). Humans built mechanical tanks (the primary component of modern armoured forces) in imitation of them. Religious significance: divine image-bearers in Tankism. The distinction between Tanks (Living Tanks) and mechanical tanks (“mechs”, or simply just “tanks”) is intuitive to most Nova Terrans.

**Calendar:** Primarily the New Era (NE) calendar first adopted in Auxur in 1 BNE in order to celebrate the rise of Auxurian power (and thus the epoch has no special significance beyond the context of Tankism; Auxur was essentially doing it to show off), the current year being 1963 NE. Leap year every four years; with an intercalary day (Midsummer's) every 100 years inserted between June 30 and July 1, which is an international holiday. Previous: 1900 NE; next: 2000 NE. Notably, in Britannia-Ireland, the year legally begins on March 25 (though Acts of Parliament are dated based on regnal years; meaning that this principally applies to the budget and fiscal years in general; in many other countries the fiscal year begins in September and in others still it’s the same as the calendar year). Attempts to approximate the 365.260119 day orbital cycle of Nova Terra, in contrast to the obscure colonial calendar (the Sol Calendar; current year 2310), which used a system where a leap year was added every four years without exception. Other, alternative calendars exist along with these as well (like the Ishanian lunisolar calendar, the Dai-Kokuese regnal calendar and the Olympian liturgical calendar based on the Sol Calendar), but they are beyond the scope of this reference document.

**Understanding of Earth:** People on Nova Terra do know Earth (here called Terra) existed, but only in an intellectual sense (and not even in an accurate one; they just know it existed, it was not Nova Terra, and that humanity specifically came from there). People on Nova Terra use “earth” as a general term to talk about their own planet or the ground on which they stand.

**Climate:** Slightly less axial tilt compared to Earth (about 10 degrees, in the opposite direction), with an eastward rotation. Little Ice Age in Columba, leading to places with supposedly maritime or temperate climates having on a regular basis white Tankmases. Slightly thicker and more oxygenated atmosphere (24%) compared to Earth, with atmospheric pressure generally being at about 1110 hPa in typical conditions.

**Note on units:** I am only using Earth units of measurements, month names, names in general, languages and days of the week as a way to convey sociocultural significance; Nova Terrans probably don’t actually use these units and names as exactly defined. The principal measurement system of Nova Terra is the decimal system, which operates units of base 10, and is usually represented using the SI system of measurements. In contrast, the Commonwealth and environs use the Commonwealth customary system, which is represented using American customary units. Many other nations have their own traditional systems of measurements (like the traditional Ishanian system of weights and measures), but this is beyond the scope of the Reference Document.

**The Great War (1914-1947):** The defining event of the 20th century, with its consequences rippling throughout history. Fought between the Second Grand Alliance (Commonwealth, Canausbel, Diplomatians (1915-), UTP (1917-1921 on Canausbelite front only; 1941-), Pusheen Country (1941-), etc.) and the Seven-Nation Army (Rapusia, Ishanis, Dai-Koku, Traroria, Qurkia (1917-), etc (largely puppet states counted for posterity purposes).), Started with the Seminal Farce - a dispute over maritime borders in the Stormy Sea between Rapusia and the Commonwealth; escalated by the unrelated assassination of the Crown Prince of Rapusia (Rudolf, an autocratic, pacifistic federalist) by a Skiyian nationalist, which Rapusia blamed on the Commonwealth - before escalating with the involvement of Canausbel, with a bloody trench-warfare meat grinder in the western Canausbelite mountains only somewhat helped by the entry of the UTP in 1917. When an armistice was reached in 1921 on the Canausbelite Front (unilaterally on the part of the Rapusian Emperor, Wilhelm V; with ambitions for world conquest but with enough pragmatism to forestall economic collapse on Rapusia’s part caused by the mismanagement of the generals given free rein to conduct the war; he seized full control in 1925 and made Rapusia a royal dictatorship that lasted until the end of the war) due to conditions of chronic shortages, famine and disease, nobody felt the war was over, and indeed there was much fighting in the intervening period (the Intrawar period), especially in Auxur (war with Rapusia, jungle warfare), Quaristanicaval (Pangarap gained independence during this time in 1929, when much of the increasingly disillusioned military mutinied and built their own state; leaving Western Quaristanicaval), Konnelia (insurgency against Ishanian suzerainty) and Monte Bianco (Rapusian efforts to conquer the country). When the ceasefire was broken in 1940 on the Canausbelite front, Rapusia successfully blitzed to Medyeva, taking it after a long siege, and invaded Pusheen Country in 1941; but overextended itself (not helping was when Dai-Koku attacked Marya Harbour in December 1941, drawing the UTP back into the war). This eventually led to the liberation of Skiyia (1944-1947) over the course of an absolutely brutal meatgrinder of slow, creeping trench warfare not too dissimilar from the Canausbelite front known as the Thousand Days’ Offensive (1944-1946), the liberation of Medyeva (July 1944), an island-hopping campaign across the Great Ocean (spearheaded by the UTP and supported by Diplomatians) and the first usage of atomic weapons in war (over the period of 1944-1947, Rapusia was nuked six times, Dai-Koku thrice and Ishanis once); leading to the unconditional surrender of the Seven-Nation Army on August 15, 1947 (in the aftermath of the Providence Memorandum of July 4, 1947, which declared that one city of the Seven-Nation Army would be destroyed with nuclear weapons per month until an unconditional surrender was obtained). Overall, approximately 185-210 million people were left dead or disabled by the Great War and its consequences. Subsidiary conflicts continue into the 1950s and 60s.

**A note on history:** Nova Terra’s history has been defined by interstate competition of every kind. Weaker nations tended to get conquered and left in the dustbin of history, only to rise again when their overlords were weakened through external conflict; it was a world where only the fittest, most adaptable nations survived. Wars between great powers were common, often devastating the minor nations they were fought in; like the Monte Biancan War (1509-1526), the various Anglo-Rapusian Wars, the Silent Revolution (1580-1582; in North Contana, establishing a republic), the various colonial Anglo-Skiyian Wars (first through third), the War of the Free Cities, the War of the Skiyian Succession (1701-1714), the eight Kannan Wars (1608-1788), culminating in the Canausbelite Revolutionary Wars. However, in “recent” centuries (“recent” being used loosely), Tankist ethics have slowly moderated this tendency, to the point that by the 18th to 19th centuries, state sovereignty was generally accepted, and declaring war purely for fun and profit was generally frowned upon (now, you have to fabricate causes for war; and even then the minor powers still did and do go to war with each other over these causes). Even the Great Powers, which are viewed as having special privileges (everyone listens to what they have to say) and responsibilities (mediating and intervening in crises in their “area of responsibility”), generally don’t oppose the idea of national sovereignty prima facie; they just view themselves as somewhat more important.

## RELIGION

### Tankism (dominant religion)
The Great Tank (also known as the Tank or simply God) is the supreme divine being: a Triune Will (Creator, Saviour and Guide of humanity), three functions of one reality; all-powerful, all-knowing and frankly a bit inscrutable despite being benevolent. Most commonly imagined in the Sherman phenotype. Living Tanks rank above humans in the cosmic hierarchy because they are made in the divine image; humans are *not* made in God's image (in fact, humans just seemingly appeared one day in Creation, and have no inherent connection to divinity beyond probably being created by the Great Tank). The worst sin is the **Sin of Silence,** failing to act against evil. Salvation requires genuine acceptance and real penance. The **Holy Septalibri** (five Trakts, two Obrakh) is the scripture. The **Tank's Prayer** is universal across denominations. **Mass (Services in Reformed denominations)** is the principal religious ceremony, held every Sunday.

**Cosmological hierarchy:** Great Tank → Twelve Holies → Living Tanks → Humans → Animals → Plants → Constructs → Objects.

**Eschatology:** The Third Coming ends the universe in a battle between Great Tank and Anti-Tank; the righteous are restored and the original Terra reconstructed.

**The Twelve Holies:** The thirteen parts of the Great Tank imbued each within an ordinary newborn Living Tanks and made to spread the Word in 1 BNE by miracles and quiet comfort, before they returned to the Great Tank on March 25, 33 NE; a day that has since become known as Tank Day (the focal day of Arising celebrations). worshipped in some denominations, merely revered in others. Iscariot, the Thirteenth, delivered small miracles and helped in less grand ways. 

**Denominations:**
- *Montagnard Orthodox:* The most ritual-heavy denomination; centred on Monte Bianco and the Archbishop of Montagna. Theologically conservative, capable of progressive social positions. The Great Convention (or Grand Synod) is something like a National People’s Congress (of Orthodox Tankist clergy), an assembly of all of the important clergy of all the bishoprics, ecclesiastical provinces and beyond in communion with the Temple of Monte Bianco, meeting every 4-6 years years to discuss ecclesiastical matters and formulate teachings based on the Septalibri. Because of its sheer size, when it is not in session, the Orthodox Tankist Temple is governed by the Curia, a cabinet of sorts elected by the Convention on the suggestion of the Archbishop of Monte Bianco. Uniquely, the Curia of the Orthodox Tankist Temple is recognised as a sovereign legal personality in its own right when the Great Convention is not in session; though when the Great Convention itself is in session, it is recognised as sovereign in its place. Common in Columba (e.g. Rapusia, Skiyia, Monte Bianco, Fillerant, etc.) and the regions of the world where Columban influence has spread greatly, though odd exceptions (like Dupeland) exist. Auxurian is the language of liturgy, in contrast to Reformed and Episcopalian temples, which use the local language.
- *Reformed:* Text-centred and decentralised with an emphasis on personal interpretation guided by discussion between the worshipful. Paradoxically produces more conservative outcomes. It itself is divided into several varieties, such as Shamalism, Orlovism and Pentecostalism. Most prevalent in the UTP (where it was founded in 1519 by Albert de Montmartre), Kingerse and North Contana (all regions relatively distant from Monte Bianco). Evangelical Tankists are generally of the Reformed type.
- *Episcopal/Anglican:* Hierarchical but independent of Monte Bianco; frequently adapts to local context. Split with the Orthodox Temple after the Reformed denomination did so during the Eutankist Schism (over the use of leavened and unleavened bread during ceremonies). Split between the High Temple (ritualistic, closer to Orthodoxy) and the Low Temple (closer to Reformism). Originated in (what would become) Britannia-Ireland, spread by its influence and trade.
- *Eastern:** Split from Orthodoxy in the 11th century; emphasises introspection and meditation; more inclined to pacifism. Also divided into denominations, the two largest being Tsmindagza (individualistic and monastic) and Martaligza (collectivist and esoteric). Found in Sharisam, Konnelia, Horonyia and southern Ishanis.
- *Southern:** Rejects the Trinity, says that the Great Tank is the one God that just so happens to have characteristics; primarily situated in western Libra. Possesses a comprehensive code of civil law based on centuries of accumulated religious jurisprudence, the Alqawaeid (the Rules). Jesusese Tankism is a notable subdenomination that believes that the Great Tank also sent one human prophet: one named Jesus Christ, based on a divine revelation where a relic of him was dug up.

**Holidays:** Tankmas (commemorating the Twelve Holies' arrival, officially on December 25 but with festivities extending from the beginning of December), Arising (their departure after 33 years, typically in late March or early April; with a whole 40 days of partial fasting beforehand).  

**Temple plan:** The floorplan usually resembles a tank turret from above, with a long corridor to the symmetrical central chamber (like the symbol of Tankism, the Turret; which can be found on jewelry and the shape of grave markers).

### Other Religions
- **Olympianism:** The polytheistic worship of the Twelve Olympians; dominant in Canausbel and Diplomatians; civic and explanatory rather than salvific. Belief lies mainly in the many, many rituals one must follow to appease the often vain and capricious deities that control the various aspects of the natural world; and perhaps going to a good afterlife by not angering them. Most Olympianists pray to whatever god that they require the assistance of in a certain situation hopefully pertinent to their domain. The three principal gods are Blitz (god of lightning and the sky), Meer (god of the sea) and Pluton (god of the underworld).
- **Faith of the Eight Virtues:** Dominant in Ishanis; with virtues of ancestor reverence, social harmony and filial piety; moral-civic tradition rather than a salvation religion. More of a belief system than a religion, so many Ishanians can be both Faithful of the Eight Virtues and followers of another religion, at least nominally (like viewing the Great Tank as the setter of the Eight Virtues), though discrimination against such syncretists isn’t unheard of, sadly.
- **Solism:** Found principally in Dai-Koku; reverent of the Sun (the source of all life), ancestry, and natural order; embedded in court and state life. Animistic in its reverence for natural concepts and places (including Living Tanks). The Emperor of Dai-Koku himself is seen as a minor deity, a descendant of the Sun Mother herself.
- **Anirniq:** The traditional religion of the Kingersean people, polytheistic and shamanistic. Believes that the entire world is one great big interconnected thing, including the wildlife; so it must be treated with care lest it feel disrespected and kill everyone (as learned by the Kingersean hunter-gatherers in the immediate aftermath of whatever event cut off Nova Terra from Terra). Taboos play a great part here.

## INTERNATIONAL ORGANISATIONS

**Community of Nations (CoN):** est. 1947, after the end of the Great War. A descendant of the Grand Alliance; an international organisation that functions as an organised diplomatic framework for the peaceful resolution of conflicts between members. The Community of Nations headquarters is located in Mons (at the Palace of Nations), but it also possesses offices in Saxenburg and Kuusaaq. The principal organs of the CoN include the Secretariat (which manages the entire project, setting agendas), the International Assembly (where each member is given one non-binding vote to adopt non-binding resolutions), the Great Council (with five permanent members: UTP, Canausbel, Commonwealth, Pusheen Country, Diplomatians; and seven others that rotate in and out every year; who manage international security by determining threats to the international order and issuing binding resolutions based on those, including sanctions), the World Court (which arbitrates legal disputes between members in the manner of a court), the Economic Council (discussing and managing economic goals such as reconstruction), the Mandate Council (which manages the Community of Nations Mandates) and Peacekeeper Command (which manages the Peacekeepers, a military force to which all CoN members contribute to varying degrees; deployed to recent conflict areas and disputes to ensure compliance to peace agreements on the orders of the Great Council). These coexist along with a variety of specialized agencies, which often existed before the CoN and were absorbed into it (like the World Bank and the Alliance for Health). Often considered inefficient, corrupt and paralyzed by procedure and the conflicting interests of its members; but generally useful at redirecting great power conflict into procedural disputes and mitigating the humanitarian effects of its diplomatic failures (sometimes). Notable successes include the 1954 intervention in Tumblria, 1958 Twittertopia humanitarian corridor and the 1959 Ataraxian refugee convoy. These, however, also come with the CoN’s many, many failures in preventing conflicts (such as the Trarori-Jesusland War of 1950-1958 or the Ataraxia-Dupeland War) and the ensuing humanitarian crises. Rapusia was excluded from permanent membership of the Great Council, and was only formally admitted to the CoN as a whole in 1954, a persistent source of resentment. Kingerse and Androsia are observers, not members. Tries to manage exploration and exploitation of Terra Antipolaris, with some success. Current peacekeeping missions include one on the border of Traroria and Jesusland, one on the border between Cavalistan and Kekistan, one in Tumblria and one in Twittertopia (though really it’s just a force occupying territory as a sanctuary for refugees). Community of Nations mandates also include the South Seas Islands (held by Diplomatians), Seylon (Rapusia), Mezong Island (Konnelia), Brzai (Commonwealth) and the entirety of Terra Antipolaris (held by nobody in particular aside from the signatories of the Antipolar Treaty of 1949; a special mandate).

**Free Nations' Alliance (FNA):** A military and political alliance largely made of managerial-capitalist and centrist democracies, which split from the wartime Grand Alliance post-Great War. Led by the UTP, and the largest bloc by GDP. Members: UTP, Britannia-Ireland, Diplomatians, Central Land, etc. Skiyia joined as a Partner (in other words, an observer, like Western Quaristanicaval) in 1952. The headquarters of the FNA (the Hexagon) is located in Central Land. The UTP bankrolls it (and provides plausible deniability); the Commonwealth provides hard military power; Diplomatians provides soft power and Central Land provides HQ and some logistical support. SHAPC (Supreme HQ Allied Powers Columba) commands forces on the Skiyian-Rapusian border under a Tankian OF-9 general. Trades and maintains cordial relationships with the International, with the goal eventually converting them to liberal democracy. Has and does take unscrupulous actions, like overthrowing governments and assassinating figures that might threaten their interests covertly, drone strikes and sponsoring ideologically amenable (centrist and sometimes leftist) regimes (including police states like Skiyia; though Skiyia chose to align with the FNA first) in part to prevent “worse” figures from taking over; though usually if they’re especially unsavoury and they’re aligned, they usually groom them into being a more palatable ally first before launching a coup.

**Taunfurt Pact:** A conservative, realist and anti-universalist bloc led by Rapusia; the second-greatest bloc by GDP. Established by the Tanfurt Pact in 1952 with two major institutions: the Zollverein and the Taunfurt Treaty Organisation. Members: Rapusia, Ishanis, Qurkia, etc. Defined more by opposition to the FNA and Third International than any particular shared vision. The TTO is the international face of the organisation, being the military alliance that keeps it all together, with collective security mechanisms and a minimum military spending requirement. The Zollverein functions as a trade bloc and monetary union, with high tariffs on imports from adversarial factions (namely, the FNA and the International) and a semi-rigid exchange-rate regime based on the Rapusian reichsmark. Culturally particularist and pro-sovereignty in justification and rhetoric (they frequently avoid the term “order” in their propaganda aimed at foreign audiences). Frequently tolerates corrupt systems in its members and allies because “it’s not their business to interfere,” and their general foreign policy is heavily defined by realpolitik; including overthrowing a democratically-elected leftist-liberal coalition government in Central Hydra in 1953 in order to install an aligned military junta there.

**Third/Socialist International:** An alliance of socialist and leftist nations of many stripes; the third-greatest bloc by GDP. Members: Canausbel, Timernond, Konnelia, Traroria, Cavalistan, Flipadossa. Reddit Island is an observer. Twittertopia has no status. Most geographically contiguous faction. Foreign policy operates on the basis of the Three-Sphere Theory: socialist allies and fellow travellers (first sphere), reformable capitalist states (second), hostile reactionary regimes to be contained (third). The bloc has collectively resolved never to discuss Reddit Island's Antiwork faction (it makes socialists look like lazy bums; even when they aren’t and actually value labour), and like the FNA supports aligned dictatorships and often pressures its members into accepting the orthodoxy of Bourquism; through subtle and (sometimes) unsubtle means. Trades and maintains cordial relationships with the FNA, with the goal of eventually converting them to socialism. Willing to use diplomatic pressure on non-members too.

**Neutrality Pact** – An alliance of generally formally neutral countries created at the 1955 Saitama Conference, which declared an opposition to power bloc competition and a support for peacefully resolving disputes via diplomacy and the Community of Nations where required; along with national sovereignty, anticolonialism and anti-imperialism. Out of the Great Powers, Pusheen Country, Dai-Koku and Auxur are its principal leading countries, and it also possesses a wide variety of members due to its broad nature; from Kekistan to Horonyia to Kannan to Ataraxia to Pangarap. However, some of its members remain aligned in practice, like Kirbistan (pro-UTP) and Ataraxia (pro-Canausbel). Frequently cooperates with the Community of Nations. Notably, Kingerse remains a non-member.

**The Great Power System:** This is less a formal organisation and more of a series of interconnected agreements and informal conventions between the nine Great Powers (the UTP, Rapusia, Canausbel, the Commonwealth, Pusheen Country, Dai-Koku, Diplomatians, Ishanis and Auxur, in descending order of GDP) as the world’s most powerful nations. The Great Power System began during the early 19th century, and has somehow managed to survive in a vestigial form to the Cold War, giving its members extra informal prestige that allows them to mediate conflicts between non-Great Power third parties in their “spheres of influence” and thus indirectly support and/or supplant the Community of Nations. However, it is only marginally less useless than the Community of Nations nowadays, a relic of a different world that people don’t even see as worth protesting; due to the Cold War… complicating matters, leaving it constrained by politics and often outright paralysed by rivalries between the Great Powers. Now, it’s principally used as an excuse for the actions of the Great Powers, rather than as an actual mechanism for mediation.

**Note on the Cold War:** The FNA and International, although ideologically adversarial, actually remain closer to neutral countries to each other; each trying to sway the other to their side when the situation comes. Both say the other is misguided, but ultimately well-intentioned to some extent, and both claim the Taunfurt Pact’s “ideology” of anti-universalism is an excuse for the continuation of oppressive systems.

## GREAT POWERS

### United Tank Provinces (UTP; Tankia)
Pop. ~208M | GDP C682bn | Per capita C3,278 | Gini 38 | Territory ~18.2M km² | Capital: Ville-Tank (Tank City) | Currency: Tankian crown (₡)

The Land of a Thousand Tanks. The most powerful country on Nova Terra militarily and economically. A federal presidential republic divided into fourteen autonomous provinces. The President, who holds executive powers, is elected every four years via the electoral college. Chamber of Deputies 435 seats, Senate 112 seats (8 per province); which together form Congress, or officially the Congress of the Parliament of the United Tank Provinces. 3 Tank City electors for presidential elections only: these electors vote with party bloc voting (where based on the results of an election in a province, the electors all vote for the candidate which garnered the most popular votes in that province), with a simple majority being enough to elect a president. The number of electors each province sends is equal to the size of its congressional delegation (senators + deputies). Rigidly bipartisan since 1952 under the current Fourth Party System, dominated by the **Nationalist Party** (social liberal, technocratic, economically interventionist; with social-democratic and trade-unionist factions) and the **Democratic Alliance** (market liberal, provincial autonomist, anti-union, assertive foreign policy, pro-free trade; a bit of an oddity in the post-Great War world as a whole). Official religion: Reformed Tankism; operates under strict laïcité. The legal system is based on Auxurian law, enforced federally by the Gendarmerie, a paramilitary civilian police institution colloquially called “mounties” despite not being mounted on horses or Tanks, along with numerous provincial and municipal police forces (that same Gendarmerie also runs the UTP’s various surveillance programs like PROCONESPI; an ongoing program to gather intelligence on radical leftists and rightists alike in order to infiltrate, subvert and discredit any “subversive organisations”). A megadiverse country including almost every single kind of landscape and species one could think of. Leader of the Free Nations’ Alliance and the leading superpower.

**Head of State (and government):** President Jean Montcalm, a Nationalist who turned the ailing Nationalists into a centrist, marketable, modernist party that appealed to the middle class while turning away from its trade-unionist roots. Also began a programme of social improvement and economic development (primarily through protectionism and economic interventionism, with rural electrification and infrastructure development being key features) called the Quiet Revolution. Also dealing with alcoholism, withdrawing Tankian forces from its Great Power allies (namely the Commonwealth and Diplomatians) in the FNA as promised by the Nationalists and the escalating Syudd Crisis. Often described as a **postliberal** due to his rejection of economic liberalism while maintaining the social and political aspects of liberalism.

**Henri Duranceau** (1953-1961): Previous president of the United Tank Provinces, the first president of the UTP from the Democratic Alliance. Strived to balance the interests of the moderate DA and the moderate Nationalists, all while maintaining the loyalty of the DA. Socially conservative and economically liberalizing, but hardly a hardliner in terms of domestic policy. Relatively hardline in foreign policy though, as evidenced by his authorisation of the kidnapping of the Prime Minister of Rafaristan, Mohammed Iravi for charges of “drug trafficking” in 1953 (this was surprisingly controversial across Nova Terra and the UTP, though noticeably less in Rafaristan itself). Popular due to his nature as a former war general until the 1958 recession, then becoming deeply unpopular for no fault of his own. Later reappraised as a fairly decent president who laid the foundation for the Quiet Revolution through his efforts to industrialise and develop the country further.

Composed of 14 provinces (the original nine joined in 1729, later additions joined through conquest or union). Possesses a single protectorate, the **Empire of Nouméa**; a volcanic island chain in the northern Great Ocean home to the UTP’s largest naval base in the region, Marya Harbour. Full nuclear triad; ~21,000 warheads (built after overestimating Rapusian arsenal at 12,000; actual Rapusian arsenal: 5,500). Foreign intelligence agency: OSIS; loyal to the government, but this is hardly a consolation to anyone affected by it and its operations. Possesses a certain reputation, massively overblown from what it already does, like overthrowing potentially unfriendly governments and placing more favourable leaders, funding opposition movements in many places, at least three false-flag operations and mind control experiments; all with a surprisingly strong amount of plausible deniability. Status quo superpower preferring economic, institutional, and covert instruments over direct military action. 

Economy: In the 16th century, the technological development of the nations that would become the UTP was comparable to that of the Near East (aka Canausbel), which were both behind Columba and East Antares. After increasing cultural contact (and having significant swathes of their land conquered by Skiyia), the UTP became roughly comparable to the West by the 1850s. Eventually, the UTP became the world’s largest economy in the 1890s, surpassing Rapusia while remaining firmly neutral and being one of the most attractive places for an immigrant to move to. At that time, the UTP was a traditional free-market economy, where the government (under the Nationalists) remained relatively hands-off while still periodically choosing to intervene to, say, do some trust-busting or impose labour regulations. That changed with the Great War causing a pivot to a new status quo: the Tankian System. Under this new order, the economy is nominally capitalist, but the power of the markets is significantly hemmed in by government regulation, state-business collusion and industrial negotiation. Tankian economic power is sustained in no small part by state intervention, protectionism and the Tankian crown, the world’s primary reserve currency, the latter of which which has been on the gold standard at a rate of 1 crown to 1 gram of gold since the end of the Great War (until June 1979, when the threat of a terminal deflation spiral was looming). General Industry is the largest conglomerate in the world (dealing primarily in manufacturing of machinery and consumer electronics), and is headquartered in Ventôse. Corporate influence is sometimes noticeable, but isn’t all-consuming precisely because the government can assert itself while still normally being a mediator between business and labour. The Tankian healthcare system mandates private insurance (often given by the employer or purchased independently), but that insurance is heavily regulated; with the government frequently providing subsidies to the poor who can’t afford those premiums and offering its own (relatively barebones) health insurance. Provincial variation in these regulations (like all regulations in the UTP) still varies, however; even if there’s a federal minimum. The federal government (as of the Affordable Health Insurance Act of 1961) has very strict regulations as to the premiums of the policies. The UTP is also the largest producer of petroleum in the world (at ~8.1M bbl/day), but much of this petroleum is consumed domestically. Currently (as of the 1960s), the UTP is showing a slightly slow growth of 4-5% due to the recovery from the recession of 1958-59; though it is expected to accelerate rapidly due to the infrastructure programs of the Quiet Revolution.

**Geography:**
The largest country on Nova Terra by land area, and home to a large population of Living Tanks that apocryphally serves as the etymology for its name. Home to almost every sort of clime and species, from the frigid (and dry) north to the hot (and dry) southwest and the hot (and humid) southeast and the relatively temperate northeast.

**A list of the provinces of the UTP, in order of joining (the first 9 were part of the original UTP established in 1729):**  

- **Tankesland:** The cultural heartland of the UTP, heavily urbanised and a bellwether for national elections due to how diverse it is; Tank City, the largest city in the world and the capital of the UTP is located here but is not part of it, being a Federal District set aside to be the national capital. Population: 41.2 million.
- **Aquitaine:** An agricultural province, subtropical, with distinctively muggy summers. Industrialising rapidly in spite of its traditional reputation as a backwater thanks to the Quiet Revolution. Population: 13.6 million.
- **Borea:** A bit more northern but still industrial, one of the largest provinces. Home to Mons, the capital of the province, a major industrial hub and host to the headquarters of the Community of Nations, the Palace of Nations. Population: 12.1 million.
- **Champlain:** Very industrial (so industrial that it has the second-largest population of any province in the country), trade unions are quite relevant here. Hometo the nation’s second city, Ventôse and a major industrial engine. Population: 41 million.
- **Languedoc:** Southeastern subtropical province, rural with urban pockets, also agricultural but rapidly industrializing, and one of the fastest growing provinces in the nation. Conservative in social mores. Population: 21 million.  
- **Lorraine:** Located smack dab in the middle of the country, also very agricultural and filled with corn fields. Nicknamed Middle Tankia for how typical it is. Population: 15.7 million.
- **Noraud:** Probably the most "boring" province, very flat, very agricultural and filled to the brim with wheat and corn fields. Population: 3 million.
- **Montagnais:** Very northern, poor for Tankian standards (which naturally means “filthy rich by Nova Terra standards”), with a fishing and resource-extraction economy. Population: 1 million.
- **Touraine:** The smallest province, on the northeastern Great Ocean coast, very culturally distinct, home to many shipyards. Built in part upon tourism. Population: 1.9 million.
- **Tejaho:** A very rich far southern province with an independent streak, typically conservative but still remains liberal, even progressive in surprising areas, conquered in 1745 from the Skiyian Empire. Stunning economic growth as of late, fuelled by industrialisation. Population: 13 million.
- **Armorica:** Coastal, rainy, forested and mountainous. Still feels like a frontier even though it’s been several centuries since it’s been an independent country. Increasingly home to high tech and manufacturing, coexisting awkwardly with the traditional mining, agriculture and fishing of the region. Population: 6 million.
- **Sierrana:** Conquered from the Skiyian Empire 1742-1745, rapidly growing to become one of the most important states, only the urban coast is famous; primarily for movie productions and surfing, home to the third largest city in the country, Los Tancas. Hosts some of the best weather in the country, with dry heat. Population: 22.4 million.
- **Firenco** – Once a major seafaring nation when it was independent, conquered in 1785, very cold, and naturally stunning. Home to great fjords and some mountains. An economy built on mining. Population: 4 million.
- **Juneau** – Actual a fusion of four former nations, including Rigel, quite rugged and cold, largely uninhabitable outside the coasts. It was conquered between 1791-1797. The economy is also built on mining. Population: 4.5 million.
- **Tank City:** Not actually a province, but the federal district that houses the nation’s largest city and federal capital (the municipality covers the same territory as the federal district but is technically not the same thing). Officially Ville-Tank, but referred to in Britannian-language sources as Tank City. According to many, the economic capital of the world. Home to landmarks like the Rose Palace (residence of the President), the Tank City Stock Exchange (also known as La Bourse; the largest securities exchange in the world by market cap), Federation Tower (the tallest building in the world), the United Provinces Capitol, Republic Square (the most famous plaza in the world) and the Statue of Unity. Affectionately dubbed the Big Apple and Metropolis. Population: 7.8 million.

Military: The UTP's military (the United Provinces Armed Forces) is built around the doctrine of Full-Spectrum Superiority, that extends the battlespace to include industrial, political and morale factors. Aims for total superiority in these areas, along with overwhelming dominance in the battlefield itself, in order to achieve strategic goals. Uses overwhelming force to harm enemy morale, allowing for an assault with relatively minimal casualties. Very competent logistics - probably the best on Nova Terra - thanks to massive efforts at standardization and interchangeability. Generally prefers perfectly serviceable equipment that’s comfortable to use and easy to train with. However, in comparison to the other two superpowers, conservatives (and certain types of progressives) often say that the United Provinces Armed Forces are underfunded in terms of % of GDP. Formerly deployed in the Commonwealth and Diplomatians, and currently deployed in Central Land and Skiyia; thanks to Nationalist efforts to scale back foreign military bases (Skiyia explicitly told the UTP not to withdraw its forces). The United Provinces Armed Forces has the smallest count of active-duty combat personnel of the three superpowers, at 2,023,000. The United Provinces Armed Forces maintains the most relaxed parade step of any military of the Great Powers. Service rifle: MAS-52, a semi-automatic battle rifle chambered in 7.62x51mm.

**Note on the Tankian space program, the National Aeronautics Institute (NAI):** Had the first non-lethal human flight 100km above the atmosphere (without orbit) in 1954 with a hypersonic plane. Launched its first man into orbit in 1959. Currently well on the way to landing a man on the moon and bringing them back home via the Pluton program.

Culture: The culture of the UTP is built upon a kind of civic nationalism, where anyone who assimilates, speaks Tankian, and follows the laws is considered part of the Tankian melting pot. You may be able to keep some, if not most of your cultural traditions, but there’s a clear expectation that it must be subordinate to Tankianness. Silent discrimination exists against those who don’t conform to Tankianness. Quiet Revolution of electrification, industrialisation and education in the rural provinces. Interestingly, the concept of a “Tankian” identity was once ethnogeographic, referencing the similarities of the original nine founding nations; before it was adapted into a civic nationalist identity after the Wars of Conquest (1735-1798) and the western insurrections that lasted into the 1840s. They also have a great appreciation for maple syrup. Commonwealth immigrants (and their descendants) form about 1.7% of the population, but already have their own stereotypes about their associations with tea, whisky and Monte Biancan organised crime. However, by far the second-most spoken language in the UTP (behind Tankian, of course) is Skiyian, spoken by about 14% of the population (especially in the southern regions); with other notable minority languages including Armorican (Breton) and Atlantean (in the region of Juneau known as Perseus). The **Tankian Dream**, first described in the 1840s (but with foundations seeded with the Homestead Act of 1737), is a recurring cultural concept that defines the Tankian spirit: it is the idea that, anyone coming from anywhere and any situation, can build a stable, prosperous life in the UTP; perhaps as the owner of a small business, an artist of some fame or some kind of worker in a stable job. Tankian culture, including music (pop, rock and jazz), art, television, film and food (including Tanka-Cola, a popular soft drink, which has since provoked many competitors domestic and foreign) has also begun to spread in the wake of the UTP’s many deployments during the Great War, with cabaret culture developing in places like Rapusia, Skiyia and beyond. The Tankian art scene is universally recognised as avant-garde, being the birthplace of futurism and a wide variety of abstract art movements. As well, conservatism and the traditional ways of doing things have begun to clash with new movements and trends that have emerged in the wake of the Great War; all on top of immense economic prosperity that seems as if it will last forever. As a result, the period of time from 1947 to the 1970s has been dubbed “Les Années Folles”: the Mad Years. Blazon of the Tankian flag: per fess, azure and gules, a plain cross argent, with fourteen stars argent in the first quarter. This flag is famous across Nova Terra, in no small part thanks to Tankian culture and its spread. 

Language: Tankian. No central regulating body; tolerant of archaisms and loanwords; has profanity-based conjugations. Based principally upon the Tankian spoken in Champlain (located in approximately the middle of the original Nine).

### Rapusian Empire
Pop. 315M (453M with colonies) | GDP C439bn (C445bn with colonies) | Per capita C1,393 | Gini ~45 | Territory ~4.8M km² | Capital: Brandenstadt | Currency: Rapusian reichsmark (ℛ︁ℳ︁)

Semi-constitutional federal monarchy (with multiple subnational monarchies and a few Free and Imperial cities, including the Kingdom of Rapusia (proper), whose King is the Rapusian Emperor; plus a very large colonial empire that is officially considered part of the Greater Rapusian Realm) in form since the end of absolutist rule in 1848. Involved in the War of the Rapusian Succession (1739-1747), which saw a coalition of foreign powers led by Skiyia seek to depose the newly-coronated Queen-regnant of Rapusia (that Queen, Marie Augusta, ended up being a fairly decent ruler by all accounts), leading to a status quo ante bellum. The legislatures are the Federal Council (upper house, made of crownland appointees) and the Imperial Diet (lower house, popularly elected in democratic elections with universal suffrage, chronically gridlocked). The Chancellor is appointed by the Emperor, but has been subject to Imperial Diet confidence since 1957. The Chancellor is almost always a neutral technocrat who has to balance factions. The military acts as de facto co-ruler during legislative deadlock, holding immense influence with an informal veto over certain government decisions, along with selectively interpreting orders (or a lack thereof) from the civilian government. Most powerful state on Columba (by a reasonably wide margin), and thus described as “fifteen countries in a trenchcoat” (the trenchcoat being the civilisational identity of “Rapusian”) due to its surprising amount of decentralization (no unified postal service, for example) and its sheer scale. Rivalries with the Commonwealth and Canausbel (the latter going back to the 13th century); viewing the former as hypocritical, vindictive snakes with too much power for a pair of tiny (in comparison to Rapusia) islands. A very diverse country in its own right, with the concept of a single Rapusian language being tenuous at best. High Rapusian (German) exists along with Low Rapusian (Low German; southern Rapusia, in the Southmark), Contanan (Dutch; northeast Rapusia), Mossovian (Polish; eastern Rapusia) and Kavkazian (Bulgarian; southeastern Rapusia), and that’s not even including all of the recognised languages present in Rapusia!  Also largely a continental country in climate, with exceptions including the southern, mountainous regions (which range from Annular in climate to very cold in the case of the southwestern Rapusian Range), with much of the country being exceedingly flat and often very good for agriculture. Orthodox Tankism is the state religion. Possesses a secret police, the Kaiserliche Geheimspolizei, that primarily concentrates on counterterrorism and such things rather than political repression; their agents wear professional suits when on duty. Leader of the Taunfurt Pact, and the second superpower.

**Emperor Wilhelm VI, of the house of Erlenburg** (r. 1949–): kindly, bumbling, devout Orthodox Tankist, technically most powerful through sole Chancellor appointment power, politically sidelined, alcoholic from stress. Inheritor of a long tradition of weirdly progressive Rapusian monarchs, via his personal sympathy to the working class (inherited from his father, Wilhelm V, who himself inherited from his own father, Karl IV). Also the scion of one of the greatest royal houses in Nova Terran history, descended from Rudolf I (the first King of Rapusia), and historically having also held the throne of Monte Bianco (and putting a cadet branch of your house in charge of another realm is no small feat on Nova Terra!). “I wish this decade could’ve been normal.”

**Fun fact about Wilhelm V:** Wilhelm V, interestingly, raised the minimum wage of Rapusia in 1926 and along with it instituted a 40-hour workweek for industrial workers. How did he get this through? He sent the army to the houses of notable conservatives in the Federal Council and Imperial Diet in order to cow them into submission.

**Head of government:** Chancellor Maximilian Von Schmidt, a rightist technocrat maintaining stability under pressure from both RNVP and SDPR. Once a caretaker chancellor after the resignation of Heinrich Schiller (served 1956-1961), the previous, centrist, populist, hellraiser chancellor who was the first commoner chancellor in Rapusia in a long time. The greatest rival of Heinrich Schiller. Growing increasingly unpopular due to alleged corrupt ties with the Marten Works, as exposed by the 1962 Pilsen Papers. His government continues to totter on, but the Empire will soon have to face its first vote of no confidence.

**Chancellor Conrad Von Hermarck** (served 1862-1891): One of the most important, and perhaps brilliant chancellors of recent Rapusian history. Cold, stern, technically brilliant in foreign affairs, but not ideal at PR and domestic issues in his aristocratic, bureaucratic and monarchist rule as Chancellor. Created a very complex system of secret alliances and guarantees that allowed Rapusia to focus on Columban affairs, including the defeat of Skiyia (1870-1875) and the notably less-successful war with Canausbel (1876-1879; Canausbel’s poor performance in that war was a notable cause of the Revolution of 1881 and dissatisfaction with the Canausbelite monarchy). Averse to maritime colonialism (unlike many in Rapusia), though he did acquire some token colonies from Skiyia after the end of that war (which the military used as staging areas to massively increase the holdings). Very authoritarian in domestic policy (with the Anti-Socialist Laws of 1878, which made him very unpopular), though he did institute the modern welfare state of Rapusia. Forced out of office due to his inability to rein in the military in Libra and disagreements with the new Emperor, Karl IV over how to deal with socialism (the Emperor wanted to negotiate; Hermarck wanted to shoot them), leading to Hermarck threatening his resignation as he had done many times previously… only for the Emperor to accept it, and boot him out of government.

**Chancellor Eduard von Reichenbach** (served 1954-1957): The Chancellor of Rapusia during the Rapusian Crisis. An unlikeable man and an often heavy-handed Chancellor who managed to suppress the unrest… with the military. He grew so unpopular in the months following the crisis that the Emperor (Wilhelm VI) elected to dismiss him himself and had a constitutional amendment rushed in that let the Imperial Diet throw out Chancellors themselves.

**Political parties:**
- *RNVP (Rapusian National People’s Party):* monarchist nationalist corporatist populists; working-class nationalists and the aristocracy united by post-Great War resentment; currently possesses a plurality (without a majority) in the Imperial Diet as of the 1961 general election (a fairly typical election for Rapusia).
- *SDPR (Social Democratic Party of Rapusia):* primary radical-social democratic opposition; anti-immigration on wages grounds; won 1983 elections, beginning détente and slow decolonisation. Generally reformist, supporting the reorientation of the economy towards trade unions and funding the welfare state while preserving the economic structure of corporatism.
- *RDP (Rapusian Democratic Party):* liberal, business-class, free market capitalist, populist, pro-immigration reform. The only party officially supporting immigration liberalisation and the end of the Gelenkte Wirtschaft. Generally seen as everyone’s second-favourite party, frequently saying perfectly sensible things while being too radical to be voted in.
- *Free Farmers’ Alliance:* single-issue, agrarian, pro-farmer. Socially conservative by default.
- *Homeland Party:* ultranationalist, indigenist, anti-corporate; supports women in military on ethnic superiority grounds; calls for "true democracy" without minority rights or rules of war. Generally seen as a bunch of kooks that just so happen to say a sensible thing now and then.

Economy: Once upon a time, Rapusia was the world’s dominant economic power; during which Rapusia became known as the heart of capitalism, despite (or maybe because) of the fact that the economy was almost entirely dominated by corporations and oligarchs. After the Great War though, the Gelenkte Wirtschaft (informal corporatism with negotiation primarily between large megaconglomerates, the military and the government, with unions being sidelined) has taken the place of the old status quo. The economy includes such industries as heavy industry, machinery, chemicals, and agriculture. The welfare state has been significantly damaged by austerity (1947-1957), but still functions; healthcare meanwhile is universal but a far cry from its pre-Great War self (and during the austerity period, actively in the process of falling apart), with cheap care but long wait times and mediocre outcomes. The Rapusian government regularly runs on budget deficits that have resulted from austerity. The Reichsmark is a primary global reserve currency alongside the Tankian crown. Somehow manages to have three industrial heartlands - one in the west (inspired by the Ruhr + Belgium; the far southwestern parts were once part of Skiyia and are essentially a shadow of their former self), one in the centre of Rapusia proper (inspired by Bohemia + Saxony; home to the Marten Works, the largest industrial conglomerate in Rapusia) and one in the east (inspired by the Donbas; the region with the most potential, and yet ravaged by the Great War and still recovering). Very tight monetary policy, which gives the reichsmark credibility as hard currency. Grows at a respectable average of 3.8% per annum as of 1958.

**Geography:** Rapusia is a federation of fifteen states, each with its own history and political system before coming together as part of a federation in 1804. Even today, Rapusia is known as the heartland of the remnants of feudalism. Those states, in approximate descending order of importance, are:

- **Rapusia:** The cultural, economic, political and demographic heart of the empire, with 162 million people. The most developed part of the empire, contributing roughly 60% of the Empire’s GDP.  
- **Mossovy:** The second-most important state of the empire, home to 80 million people, located in the east. Largely rural, but with significant urban and industrial areas.  
- **Ostmark:** An important state, home to much of the Empire’s agricultural production (and is indeed one of the most important agricultural regions in the world). Mostly flat, allowing it to accommodate 23 million people.  
- **Kavkazia:** A major industrial and agricultural region; but mostly known for mining.  
- **Erdmaria:** One of the poorest, most backwards regions of Rapusia (located to the southeast of Kavkazia; right next to the border with Canausbel), right up there with Lippenia. Principally an area of agriculture and resource extraction.  
- **White Rapusia:** An increasingly industrial region located just to the east of Rapusia.  
- **Lippenia:** Largely hilly with pockets of flatland, this is the archetypal land of the backwards Rapusian. Largely agricultural. Borders Canausbel.  
- **Kuybania:** A principally agricultural region to the west of Kavkazia.  
- **Contana:** Located along the Contanan Sea, a disproportionately developed region of Rapusia. The government is turning this region into a technologically-oriented land (being the current hub of computer development in Rapusia).  
- **Sudmark:** Home to much of Rapusia’s oil reserves, and mostly flat aside from the border with Rapusia to the northeast and Lippenia to the east. Borders Canausbel.  
- **Uridia:** A small borderland with Pusheen Country. Principally based on lumber and mining.  
- **Goteland:** A naval-inclined region located on the coast on the Oculus. Also a major internal tourist destination for its beaches.  
- **Ingrenburg:** The northernmost big city of the country, located at the end of a gulf of the Contanan Sea. Population: 3 million.  
- **Ersenburg:** The Empire’s primary naval base on the Oculus.  
- **Franzhaven:** The Empire’s *other* naval base on the Oculus. Also a bit of an industrial city too.  

**The Four Sisters:** The Four Sisters are the four great megacorporations of Rapusia, each named after the family that owns them (even if some are nominally publicly listed on the Brandenstadt Stock Exchange). They are composed of the Marten Group (vehicles, steel and arms), IG Meyer (pharmaceuticals and chemicals), Geisel (energy) and Hoffman (electrical equipment). Rapusische Banke and Rapusische Energie, although almost as big as them, are not counted due to being a financial institution owned in large part by the state and a state-owned corporation respectively.

Military: The Wehrmacht is designed principally for fast decisive campaigns, not for wars of attrition (though they are still capable of those thanks to sheer scale), with a decaying advantage curve. Fully mechanised post-Great War. Service rifle: Gewehr 52 (select-fire, based on captured C1A1, rechambered in 7.92×57mm). Flecktarn camouflage since 1944. However, Great War surplus such as the Gewehr 99 (a straight-pull bolt-action) and the world's first assault rifle, the Modell 41, remain in common use in the Internal Troops, reservists, colonial units, marines and much of the rear-echelon units of the Army. Underestimate at your own peril. Invented the world’s first IFVs, the KfZ.225 half-tracks (still in use), during the Great War; before innovating in making fully tracked IFVs. The Stahlhelm helmet has been continuous since 1916. Highly efficient intelligence and disinformation apparatus. However, Rapusia has a tendency to make very complicated machinery, which can either work stunningly well or be a maintenance nightmare (sometimes both). Possesses a Stormtrooper Corps (part of the Army, established during the Great War from the lessons of the early years of the Great War); divided into battalions of shock troops (each attached to a division) trained to infiltrate enemy positions at great risk and provide support for armoured forces; though they are also capable of being spearheads of infantry operations in less tank-conducive terrain like cities and jungles. Unlike regular infantry, they are enthusiastic users of machine pistols (submachine guns). Possesses the world’s third-largest navy (the Imperial Rapusian Navy), primarily aimed at tying up the Commonwealth Navy in the Stormy Sea with its very capable U-boat force. The Air Force is well adapted to close air support, and has learned a thing or two about strategic bombing and independent operations. A demilitarised zone extends along the border with Pusheen Country, though it widens massively around far-northeastern Mossovy before it encompasses the entirety of Uridia. **Destroyer fact:** Every single destroyer of the Imperial Rapusian Navy is assigned a designation on the model of Z(number) and a name (usually a notable naval hero) like Z33 Johannes Maruer (of the Z20 class), which was the only naval unit to mutiny in 1956 (and even then, they only refused to comply with operational orders; they would still patrol Rapusian waters and fight any enemy which encroached upon Rapusia’s territorial borders). The Wehrmacht is also the largest military in the world by active-duty combat personnel, with 2,458,000 in total. 

Culture: Despite stereotypes suggesting otherwise, Rapusians do have a sense of humour. It is often extremely dry and delivered through ridiculous (and often dark) anecdotes. Also, Rapusians (and Rapusia) are the primary targets of jokes about empire and post-imperial hangovers. Although suspicious of anything outside the official ethnic groups of Rapusia, the identities of those official ethnic groups are frequently embraced by state propaganda as a counter to Tankian rhetoric. Many of the non-Rapusian populations of the empire (like the Mossovians and the Contanans) do actually like being part of the Empire, and consider themselves Rapusian in addition to their ethnic identity (though Pusheenian troops massacred and raped Mossovians during their counteroffensive in the waning years of the Great War, so at least in their case it might just be a case of “the enemy of my enemy is a friend”). They also are the principal base of the RNVP’s support. Alcoholism is often associated with Rapusians (with a grain of truth; Rapusia is the world’s largest consumer of beer and vodka) due to the trauma of the Great War. Rapusia is also a major producer of caramel bricks (Kaisercaramell being the official imperial supplier); sold to the general public and internationally as an enduring cultural symbol of Rapusia. The national flag is a horizontal black-blue-white tricolour with a square gold canton with the black imperial eagle, wide and tall as two stripes.

**Note on the Rapusian space program, the Rapusian Imperial Air and Space Administration (RIASA):** Launched the world’s first useful(-ish) satellite in 1956, Schwalbe 1, which provided useful data on weather patterns, did radio telemetry experiments and took some nice images of Nova Terra from orbit. Later, launched the first human into orbit that returned alive with Adler 1 in 1958 (with 5 more missions over the next couple of years), and eventually the first survivable spacewalk on Falken 2 in 1962. Optimism abounds when it comes to the space program in Rapusia!

**Note on the Rapusian colonies:** In the colonies (more specifically in Realm-Commission Libra that manages the Libran colonies), the military acts more independently from the government in Brandenstadt; having massively expanded Rapusia’s holdings in the region in the late 1880s without the consent of Parliament, the Chancellor or even the Emperor. However, the Wehrmacht has recently been placed under a tighter leash by the central government after several notable incidents of excessive violence in suppression of colonial uprisings. However, these practices still continue to a lesser extent (now with the approval of the metropolitan government). 

**A note on paramilitary organisations:** Some paramilitary organisations exist in Rapusia; largely right-wing ones called Freikorps and their associate organisations, which were once used by the government to suppress unrest in the wake of the end of the Great War (the Machtkampf from 1947 to about 1951). They were also used to circumvent the 10-year arms restrictions placed on Rapusia in the aftermath of the Great War. However, they are largely viewed with suspicion by the political class and the population due to their historical brutality and… peculiar… politics, and were marginalized in the early 1950s. Some veterans of the Freikorps ended up becoming RNVP supporters, while others ended up founding the Homeland Party.

**A note on Rapusian rearmament:** Rapusia, after the Treaty of Tank City (which ended the Great War) in 1948, was placed under international supervision. Its army would be reduced in size to 800,000 volunteer active-duty personnel, significant portions of its navy (including all the aircraft carriers and most of the modern battleships) and merchant marine would be handed over to the Allies, Rapusia would have to make minor (but notable) territorial concessions in the metropole and colonies (losing something like 20% of her empire), and she was slapped with massive (but probably repayable) war reparations. However, the UTP allowed some concessions to Rapusia: known dual-use technologies would be allowed, like nuclear power; and significant portions of the war reparations to the UTP were annulled “in the interest of forgiveness…” The RNVP, which had much power by this point, exploited the complacency of the International Control Council that was intended to supervise it by lying that its rhetoric about “rebuilding Rapusian greatness” was just for show (when in fact, that was exactly what they were doing). In 1953, a bright flash and a mushroom cloud were spotted off the Stormy Sea coast of Rapusia. Although the official cause remains undetermined, many saw it as a Rapusian nuclear test in contravention of the Treaty, and the Commonwealth, Canausbel and Pusheen Country left the ICC in protest. However, due to Rapusia’s power and prestige (and their own war exhaustion), they couldn’t actually do much to stop them from rearming in “secret” (really, it was an open secret), and by the Rapusian Crisis of 1956, it was but a legal fiction. By the time the restrictions were officially lifted in 1958, Rapusia already had a sizable nuclear arsenal (and already Canausbel was preparing its own to counter it), the Navy was the third-largest in the world, and the Army was ready for its ranks to swell massively with over 600,000 activated paramilitary personnel mobilized in secret.

**The Rapusian Crisis of 1956:** In the face of austerity and declining quality of life, many Rapusians took to the streets and demonstrated relatively peacefully in May 1956. Strikes, sit-ins, walkouts and in one case a mutiny were some expressions of this tendency; though localized riots occurred and were quickly suppressed by the local police using tear gas and riot gear. Although these protests were initially allowed to continue unimpeded, some radical factions (like the banned Independent Social-Democratic Labour Party) seized government buildings, leading to much more violent riots that in some areas caused complete and utter anarchy; which criminals exploited to great effect. At this point, the decision was made to violently suppress all these demonstrations using the military, leading to great loss of life (possibly costing the lives of upwards to 4,000 protestors across the Empire). These protests, however, did lead to the passing of a constitutional amendment in 1956 that made the Chancellor accountable to the Imperial Diet via motions of no confidence (though at this point the Diet would not be electing the Chancellor by any means for several decades).

### Canausbelite Republic
Pop. ~295M | GDP C373bn | Per capita C1,264 | Gini 28 | Territory ~11.7M km² | Capital: Medyeva | Currency: Canausbelite lira (₺)

Semi-presidential unitary republic. The President is directly elected by the population in elections every 5 years, the Premier is appointed by that President from the House of Representatives, and the National Assembly is  bicameral (Senate, House of Representatives). The **Canausbelite Workers’ Party** dominates within a functioning, if heavily tilted, political order with real institutions and an opposition (principally composed of the National Democrats; conservatives; and the Liberal Party), along with a multitude of other parties that are nevertheless often gatekept from power. Not a fully liberal democracy, instead being built upon the principles of Bourquism and “people’s democracy.” Attempting to open up, economically speaking; with tourism and commerce becoming easier in comparison to earlier decades. Disparities between urban and rural areas, though, do exist, and are addressed through a system of regions that do some of the things that federal units do in other nations (without actually being federal units). Generally warm and dry climate (and mountainous, or plateau), with swamps in the south and temperate and dry weather (and steppe) towards the north, and indeed one of the largest countries in the world. Rivals with Rapusia since the 13th century; established through the Canausbelite Wars of Unification (1247-1491), an almost quarter-millennium of confused fighting between what would be Canausbel and the other post-Ishanian warlord states. Leader of the Third International and the socialist world; the third superpower.

**Canausbelite Revolutionary Wars (1789-1815):** Began after the Canausbelite Revolution in 1788 overthrew the Canausbelite monarchy and established a revolutionary republic (that later grew to be an attempted totalitarian regime under the rule of the Council of Elders of the Revolution after 1799), and in response a significant portion of Columba formed a coalition against Canausbel. After a series of military conflicts that consumed much of Nova Terra, including Ishanis and Dai-Koku taking the opportunity to fight again for like the fifth time in recorded history, and Rapusia and the Commonwealth had the Fourth Anglo-Rapusian War of 1812-1815 (which was a naval war over the Commonwealth’s right as a neutral nation to trade with Canausbel, predictably the Commonwealth won), along with the war of the anti-Canausbelite coalition against Canausbel, the Canausbelite monarchy was restored in 1815, but as a parliamentary monarchy. The first modern war of Nova Terra due to Canausbel’s innovation in specifically conscript-based armies (rather than mass levies or professional armies; this proved unpopular in Canausbel in the 1790s leading to many revolts), and defined what Nova Terra thought was until the Great War. Living Tank use peaked here. Even if republicanism was suppressed for the time being, it eventually came back with a vengeance, leading to the 19th century after this becoming known as the Century of Revolutions, often aimed at restricting the powers of monarchies or even establishing republics, like Rapusia in 1848, Diplomatians in 1864, Qurkia in 1871, Canausbel again in 1881 and Qurkia again in 1911. The failures of the Council of Elders informed later efforts to launch a revolution in Canausbel.

**Bourquism:** The ruling ideology of Canausbel; which emphasizes democratic, participatory socialism as an ongoing *process* heading to a socialist mode of production (collective ownership of the means of production), not a single event. The dominant socialist tendency on Nova Terra, so dominant that when a politically informed Nova Terran speaks of socialism, this is generally what they’re talking about. Exists due to the previous failure of more radical ideologies during the Canausbelite Revolutionary Wars that didn’t try to embed themselves in the population. Believes that the day the revolution is completed is the day that socialism is considered normal rather than an ideology that has to be consciously promoted. Deeply legalist; ehich explains why markets still exist, why co-ops are tolerated (and are in fact endorsed for the time being) and why the 1881 constitutional shell persists. Also provides infinite justification for postponing liberal democratisation, but a typical Canausbelite theorist would say that would be a bourgeois conception of democracy (contrasting it with the CWP’s democracy, where the workers are given a say and conveniently vote for the CWP). Bourquism also accepts national particularities in the path to socialism, because if they're trying to achieve socialism, then they're already aligning with the historical process anyway. And of course, Canausbel often pressures smaller nations to conform to the Bourquist orthodoxy anyway (sometimes for the better, like when they're prodding Flipadossa to democratize). Formally adopted as the ideological basis for modern Canausbel via a constitutional amendment ratified in 1945. 

Economy: Once upon a time, Canausbel was known as the Sick Man of Nova Terra in the immediate wake of the Revolutionary Wars. It would not have been a description entirely without merit, either, as towards the 1860s and 1870s the country grew increasingly stagnant after showing promising growth in the 1840s and 1850s during the beginnings of industrialisation. Nowadays (after… quite a lot of things have happened), Canausbel’s economy is a planned mixed economy, dubbed a Socialised Economy in Bourquist terminology. The state controls energy, heavy industry, transport, and banking; the means of production. Consumer markets, small businesses, and co-ops remain. Sectoral councils coordinate production at the highest levels. Canausbel is the **second-largest oil producer on Nova Terra (~5.9 million bbl/day)**, but is in practice the largest net exporter and thus a major swing producer. Currency: The Canausbelite lira, after four post-Great War redenominations (Paper Lira → New Lira → Dollar → Pound → current Lira 1950); exchange rate from Paper Lira to current: ~1:1,000,000,000. Canneft is the state-owned oil corporation. Home to the Velimovsk Canal (built in 1868 with Commonwealth money and expertise, but fully sold to Canausbel in 1956 after a long period of it being run as a concession to the Velimovsk Canal Company, which was 50% owned by the Commonwealth government), on the Isthmus of Velimovsk, that serves as the primary maritime route between the Annulus and the Ataraxian Sea. A major industrial, scientific and agricultural power, with Canausbelite weapons being renowned for durability and the Fertile Crescent/Corn Belt and Ataraxia Delta in particular being one of the most prosperous agricultural regions on Nova Terra. GDP growth remains solid at an annual 6-7% as a part of the process to catch up to the UTP and Rapusia.

Military: The unified Canausbelite Armed Forces (established 1949) with Strategic Rocket Forces, Ground Forces, Air Defence Forces, Air Forces and the Navy (the last one being the “runt” of the Armed Forces, being often passed over for new funding; before a major Rapusian naval rearmament in 1959 put an end to that; the 4th or 5th largest depending on the year). The Air Force is a well-rounded airforce, capable of almost anything Stavka tells them to do, from close air support to strategic bombing and everything in between. The military is a major national identity pillar (Medyeva was occupied 1940–44), and Canausbel is indeed about as militaristic as one would expect from a country on Nova Terra. Uses the operational principles of Operational Depth, a doctrine that exploits the mountainous terrain of northern Canausbel in order to weaken invaders by attrition and partisan activity, before infantry units strike back. Due to the constant looming threat of Rapusia, the Canausbelite Armed Forces has a count of active-duty personnel at near-parity to the Wehrmacht, at 2,447,000. An early adopter of the assault rifle; using the Type 45, chambered in 7.62x39mm.

**Foreign policy:** Functions on the basis of the Three-Sphere Theory (devised by Canausbelite political economist and Bourquist Antonin Morozow; the very same one who invented GDP and GNP as concepts): socialist allies (first sphere), reformable capitalist states (second), hostile reactionary regimes to be contained (third). Maintains comfortable trading relations with capitalist countries. Unusual Commonwealth relationship: historical goodwill + shared hostility to Rapusia produces occasional cooperation despite Cold War opposition. Covertly supported the Commonwealth in Syudd against permanent-revolution insurgents (deemed revisionists) while publicly maintaining anti-colonial posture. Strong rivalry with Rapusia, dating back to the unifications of Canausbel and Rapusia proper in the 13th century. Delivers an enormous amount of aid (economic and military) to International members under the Morozow Doctrine of containing reactionary states by supporting their neighbours.

Culture: Olympianist majority in terms of religion, but the state remains officially secular (since socialism doesn’t say anything about religion), with significant Orthodox Tankist and secular minorities. **Revolution Day** (March 29) is the end of a week-long festival; teenagers use it to ask out crushes. The **1881 Overture** (composer Lukas Zhigurov, who considered it somewhat glurgey) is a defining national musical symbol, as an orchestral piece about the Second Canausbelite Revolution. "Comrade" is a formal CWP marker, not a universal social marker. Frequently festive in mood even in “winter.” Canausbel is also relatively ethnically homogenous. Canausbelites are stereotyped as hard-drinking party animals, drinking for almost every occasion, good and bad. Also possesses many sports that aren’t very popular elsewhere, like its own variety of gridiron (which eventually made it over to the UTP when Canausbelite immigrants brought it there during the 19th century). The flag of Canausbel is divided per pale, gules with a castle of gold mauled saber and azure rinse and argent.

**Note on the Canausbelite space program, the Canausbelite Space Agency (CSA):** Launched its first man into orbit in 1960, a week before the Commonwealth. In the process of developing a space station, in the Nebolaboratoriya program, in order to show that socialism can still innovate for the sake of developing human knowledge as a whole rather than being stuck on earth doing economic things. A leader in biological experiments in space.

**Current concern:** Premier Anton Poroshenko is attempting to bypass democratic institutions; Bourquist gradualism provides theoretical justification for every tightening of control. The right-wing opposition exploits this by emphasizing minority rights and liberal conceptions of democracy, in contrast to Bourquist collectivism and Canausbelite nationalism. However, protests still occur against his rule without being violently repressed, dissent within the CWP exists, and Canausbelite civil society continues to function.

**Heads:** President Bryson Aeltia (populist; president from 1957 onwards) and Premier Anton Poroshenko, a centrist within the CWP, the aforementioned wannabe authoritarian, and only recently the premier; since 1962 after a brief power struggle in the aftermath of the death of the late giant of Canausbelite politics, Javis Mendriks; first premier of Red Canausbel.

### Commonwealth of Britannia-Ireland
Pop. 72.7M (93M with colonies) | GDP 164bn (C193bn including colonies) | Per capita C2,250 | Gini 30 | Territory ~315k km² | Capital: Lundburgh | Currency: pound sterling (£sd, non-decimal) |

Federal parliamentary constitutional monarchy, a real union of the Kingdom of Britannia and the Principality of Ireland, reigned over by the President. A deeply romantic nation in how it tells its stories of futile last stands and the such, but deeply unromantic in the way it seeks to avoid reproducing last stands. The presidency of the Commonwealth (as a confederal entity) is a hereditary, monarchical office universally treated as a monarchy despite the naming convention (seriously, they’re addressed as His/Her Majesty). Four de jure offices (President, King/Queen of Britannia, Prince of Ireland, Lord Protector of the Temple of Britannia) are automatically bundled with the monarchy, currently held by Elizabeth III (r. 1955-). Real executive power rests with the Prime Minister (by convention, not law), typically the leader of the largest party in the Commons after a general election; though the only real requirement is that they must be able to maintain the confidence of Parliament. Technically, the only office they formally hold is the title of Lord Chancellor (the guy who keeps the Great Seal and advises the monarch on judicial appointments, in practice delegated to the Speaker of the Senate). The First Lord of the Treasury/Chancellor of the Exchequer is a separate person appointed on the advice of the prime minister responsible for the finances of the realm). The House of Commons (652 members) is the de facto supreme legislative body of the Commonwealth thanks to the doctrine of parliamentary supremacy. The Senate (305 appointed members, replaced the Lords 1909), the descendant of a long aristocratic tradition (where all nobles are equal, and so each get one vote), can only fully block laws changing election dates; but otherwise can block laws up to three times before it must be passed. The Appellate Committee of the Senate (Law Senators) is the final court of appeal. Primary user of hard power in the FNA. The climate of the Commonwealth of Britannia-Ireland is generally marked by warm summers and mild and snowy winters, with heavy precipitation throughout. The state religion is Episcopalian (Anglican) Tankism, with the monarch being the supreme governor of the Britannian Temple. Member of the FNA, sometimes considered a quasisuperpower.

**On the origins of the union:** A union of two co-constituent nations, those being the Kingdom of Britannia; itself ceremonially made of Anglia, Scotia and Gallia, and the Principality of Ireland, which was promoted to this upon unification, having previously been a High Kingdom. Both assert continuity with a pre-unification identity. The union was established by the Treaty of Porte-de-Galles in 1603 (itself reached after the High King of Ireland had the throne of Britannia passed to him from his cousin, the Queen of Britannia, who died in 1601), and not a unitary state. Ireland retains its own Oireachtas and Taoiseach; but Britannia does not have anything equivalent. 

**Political parties:** The dominant political parties are the Conservative Party (more pro-free trade, more restrained foreign policy, more aristocratic), the Labour Party (populist, militaristic and social-democratic; proudly nationalist, though some factions want to preserve the status quo, and others want to make the transition to a fully socialist system; supported by industrial workers and some intellectuals) and the Liberal Party (technocratic, social-liberal, supported by urban professionals). These three parties (along with the national-liberal National Democrats) were once part of the UNITY coalition that ruled the Commonwealth from 1948 to 1961, which instituted the current economic status quo. Other parties include Fine Ghaedhal (Irish nationalists, conservative big tent, in a confidence and supply agreement with the Tories) and the National Renewal-Social Credit alliance (the National Renewal party advocates for authoritarian non-partisan governance in the vein of Sanationism; the Social Credit party supports social credit and opposes central banking).

**Ralph Livingston** (prime minister 1940-1947 and 1955-1959): Liberal prime minister of the Commonwealth during the final years of the Great War. Intended to be a compromise between the opposition, the loyalist Conservatives and the dissident Conservatives (all of whom were fighting in the aftermath of the fall of Medyeva and Monte Bianco, which the Tory government of Augustus Clay spectacularly mismanaged). In complete defiance of Liberal policy up to this point, he turned the Commonwealth back into a state dedicated to total war, led the country to victory, and carried the Liberal Party to second place in the postwar 1947 general election via his wartime performance (beating the Conservatives; relegating them to third-party status for the first time in history!), although he himself had resigned beforehand. He ended up appointed for a second term when the Liberals were part of the UNITY coalition, during which he oversaw the continuance of the UNITY coalition’s dirigisme and towards the end of his term the war in Twittertopia. However, due to ill health, he resigned in August 1959. He was well known for his oratorical skill, as evidenced in his Defiance speech of August 1940.

**Henry IX, of the house of Stewart** (r. 1936-1955): Monarch of the Commonwealth for the last part of the Great War and the opening years of the Cold War. Fondly remembered due to his closeness to his people and his frankness, staying in Lundburgh throughout the Great War and regularly taking walks through the city unescorted and unannounced. A heavy smoker, who eventually died of lung cancer, passing the throne to Elizabeth III (his daughter) and plunging the country into a period of deep mourning. Probably the snarkiest monarch in Nova Terran history. “Getting bombed really lets you have a feel for being an East Ender. Even if it really doesn’t.” (He said, in 1940)

**Administrative structure and notable territories:** Five territorial tiers exist: Britannia-Ireland proper (which is itself divided into counties), Crown colonies, Crown dependencies, Dominions and Protectorates; the latter three not being sovereign territory of the Commonwealth of Britannia-Ireland. **Atlantis** is a Crown colony (conquered 1683; with an endangered but not yet extinct culture), located on a mid-Central Ocean island almost as big as Ireland alone. Once upon a time, it was a major power almost capable of rivaling Auxur, before its western territories (in what is now the UTP) were conquered in the 200s, leading to a long decline accelerated by a great storm that sank a significant portion of the island in about 752 (mythologized in Tankism as the Second Coming of the Great Tank), before the Commonwealth ultimately conquered it in 1683. **Heunggong** is another small colony owned by the Commonwealth in a 999-year lease from Ishanis (which was originally a 99 year lease beginning in 1898, before the Commonwealth pressured Ishanis into extending it for another 900 years; the fact that it wasn’t extended by 999 years was a small victory for the Ishanian diplomats); a prosperous and distinctive metropolis (by Ishanian standards) in its own right that is nevertheless technically a colony with its own Ishanic language. **Reichshafen** is another crown colony, the largest in the Hydran Sea region, being a former Rapusian colony ceded to the Commonwealth after the Great War; and itself being a former Skiyian colony before 1875. Atlantis, Reichshafen and Heunggong together make the vast majority of the GDP and the population of the Commonwealth’s colonial empire, with their primary industries being manufacturing; unusual for colonies on Nova Terra. Assorted other former Skiyian territories in the north Central Ocean were also seized by the Commonwealth in the aftermath of the fall of Skiyia in 1875. **United Mayara** (Malaya; a federation of monarchies under a single king elected by an electoral college of kings; dealing with a socialist insurgency; Capital: Simunsu), **Banwadda** (Brunei), **Tikuo** (Sarawak; unusual in that it is ruled by a dynasty of white people, the Smith family, who were granted rule of this territory in order to combat pirates, and have retained popularity) are protectorates (with separate heads of state; Commonwealth interests are represented through the resident High Commissioner instead). Its colonial empire as a whole is much smaller (being largely a collection of coastal enclaves and islands) due to its primary reason to exist being as naval bases, resource territories and denial to other powers; with more lenient administrations (best described as liberal autocracies) and a lack of inclination towards granting colonies home rule unless they’re like Nova Scotia.

The **Free State of Nova Scotia** (geographically New Zealand) is a Dominion (shared head of state but independent domestic policy) with a population mostly descended from white settlers, but a decent relationship with the natives exists; autonomy was granted to it in 1901. GDP per capita C2,646. A very good place to live in its own right, with a healthy political landscape (dominated by the Labour and Union parties) and a decent agricultural economy (probably the only developed agricultural country in this world). Capital: Port Hawkesbury.

Economy: dirigiste, including Five-Year Plans. Defence spending 13.2% GDP (the most militarised of the Great Powers, with Pusheen Country coming in second), as the FNA's primary hard power contributor. Miracle on the Thassen thanks to said Five-Year Plans (where the economy regularly grows 7-8% a year, an astonishing number for a developed nation). Invented what was essentially Keynesian demand management through wartime necessity. Ready to pivot to a war economy at a moment’s notice. Strong free-trade aversion: strategic industries must be domestic in the Commonwealth’s political imagination. Railways are gauged 1435mm (Britannia) and 1600mm (Ireland), which are incompatible gauges; standardisation (even with the rest of the world, which uses 1473mm as a gauge) never pursued due to cost considerations and practicality. The shilling (1/20 of a pound) is the principal unit of account for foreign trade, while the pound is the principal unit of account domestically (which exploits a property of exchange rates that makes exports cheaper if the item is denominated in a unit of lower value).

Military: The Commonwealth Armed Forces (comprising the Royal Commonwealth Naval Service, the Commonwealth Army and the Commonwealth Air Force) are principally optimised for wars of attrition, convoy defence, night fighting and power projection. Possesses the second-largest navy in the world (the Royal Commonwealth Navy) and one of the largest air forces in the world; with 1,204,000 active-duty combat personnel overall. The Army is viewed as less important in strategic thinking, being only at most about 720,000 strong. Three tiers of personnel exist: regular volunteers, conscripts and the Home Guard (unpaid militia, never stood down since the Great War). Famous night-fighting capability (radar-guided approach, followed by searchlight-aimed gunfire). One such incident would be the Battle of Cape Musandam (1940): where a small Commonwealth task force of a few battleships, several cruisers and an aircraft carrier destroyed an entire Qurkian task force of state-of-the-art, Rapusian-built heavy cruisers in ~10 minutes. On a tactical level (universally, actually), values overwhelming amounts of firepower. The Commonwealth is one of the major users of sea forts: artificial structures placed offshore and occupied by Army and Navy forces to guard against any potential intruders; these forts form part of the greater “Island Fortress,” a systematic effort to make the Commonwealth extremely difficult to invade with the construction of tunnel networks, bunkers and pre-prepared guerilla cells. Uniforms are primarily grey; red for Hussar/Guard regiments and green for Irish units; rogatywkas are common in non-frontline roles and ceremony. The Commonwealth marching step in ceremony has diverged from the version of it adopted by the Tankians by taking it and making it a very, very slight goose-step, while still being extremely restrained. The service rifle is the C1A1 SLR, a select-fire battle rifle in 7.2x43mm, along with the No. 9 bullpup assault rifle (also in 7.2x43mm). 

Culture: The Commonwealth is, quite obviously, officially bicultural (Britannian and Irish). An unofficial Commonwealth culture emerged post-Great War, universally acknowledged but never sanctioned. Stoic, darkly funny; feelings preferably expressed through small gestures that hint at much, much stronger feelings and deadpan humour. Legendary stubbornness. ~36–60 sunny days per year. Rapusian minority in East Anglia facing historic and ongoing discrimination (Test Act 1653, Penal Laws 1689–1829, only fully lifted de jure 1994). Strong rivalry with Rapusia; viewing them as arrogant, deflective imperialists (due to various wars fought between them from the First Anglo-Rapusian War of 1574-1578 to the Fourth Anglo-Rapusian War of 1812-1815; the latter being often dubbed the “Tariff War” despite being about the neutral Commonwealth trading with Canausbel, where the RCN whooped the Imperial Rapusian Navy in the backside but was content to leave it at that). A legal right to bear arms exists, but remains regulated by the government (with the more modern your firearm gets, the less likely it is that your license application will be approved). The general attitude to this in the Commonwealth is “the government better have a good reason to tell me no,” rather than anything particularly rights-based. As a result, owning a musket or a flintlock for home defense isn’t seen as an oddity; more as a family heirloom with practical utility, especially in rural areas. This is one of the quirks of the Commonwealth that gets exaggerated in foreign political satire. Traditional Commonwealth architecture often has thick masonry with heavily sloped roofs and a relatively subdued palette of grey, dark sandstone and brick red/brown, though influences from foreign architecture have made it to the Commonwealth. The de-facto flag of the Commonwealth is well-known for being poorly designed and overcomplicated, being a red ensign with a Union Jack (defaced with the arms of Ireland, without the cross of St. Patrick) in the canton and the shield of the Commonwealth on the fly side. However, it remains very distinctive and famous across Nova Terra, due to the Commonwealth’s extensive trade routes.

**Note on the Commonwealth space program, the Commonwealth Royal Space Administration (CRSA):** Launched its first manned mission in 1960, and later accomplished the first orbital rendezvous in 1962 in a joint mission with the UTP (Royal 6-Centurion 6).

**Syudd** was a failed counterinsurgency against radical agrarian socialist insurgents (the People’s Democratic Liberation Front of Syudd) in a former colony (1964-1977), generating a postwar stab-in-the-back myth due to its consequences (a brutal dictatorship that managed to kill off 30% of Syudd’s population via forced agrarianisation and ethnic cleansing before Rapusia liberated it in 1980) and a more embittered, unilateral Commonwealth foreign policy. However, even before this, the Commonwealth had an independent and often proactive foreign policy; only sometimes aligning with the interests of the UTP (case in point, the War in Twittertopia).

**Head of government:** Prime Minister Robert Thatcher, a populist one-nation conservative (supporting the welfare state while still being conservative and nationalist; out of the belief that the better-off ought to help the less well-to-do); oddly accepting of LGBTQ+ people once he meets them personally. A relatively moderate nationalist focused on negotiating a peaceful withdrawal from Syudd after it gained formal independence and trying to get the Commonwealth to pivot to domestic and Columban affairs. Unfortunately, history has other plans.

### Pusheenian Empire (Pusheenia; or colloquially Pusheen Country)
Pop. ~131M | GDP C154bn | Per capita C1,172 | Gini 35 | Territory ~8.2M km² | Capital: Pusheenigrad | Currency: Pusheenian hryvnia

Nominally a unitary parliamentary semi-constitutional monarchy; a repressive autocracy and a sham democracy in practice. **Emperor Nikolai IV** holds the real power, and uses it with extreme prejudice. The legislative Verkhovna Rada, even if debate does occur (which it does, regularly), primarily legitimises rather than challenges, with a real but heavily restrained opposition. The Security Service monitors dissidents, socialists, republicans, and especially the Rapusian minority (facing harsh discrimination, repression, and cultural erasure). Official neutrality: armed, defensive, stubborn, and distrusts both Rapusia (invaded 1941) and the UTP, and sometimes finds itself aligned with Canausbel due to cultural similarities. Technically has a very, very large coast on the Boreal Ocean, but it frequently freezes over, and even when it isn’t frozen, it’s cold (“a warm-water port” is a Pusheenian idiom for something seemingly unattainable that others are endowed with). The state religion is Orthodox Tankism.

**Emperor Nikolai IV, of the house of Ridman (r. 1922-present):** The scion of an old family that were once known as the Hetmans of the region, Nikolai IV is a polarizing figure in much of the world. To most Pusheenians, he’s a stern but wise leader who led them to victory in the Great War and has made Pusheen Country a strong and prosperous nation, like a father to them. To many others, he is a tyrannical dictator with a cult of personality he cultivates himself. And to others still, he is a cynical pragmatist, doing whatever it takes to maintain his popularity with the people and the elites he reigns over. Also good friends with Petro Kramar, attending state functions together and reading much the same books. Notably, Nikolai IV is a “confirmed bachelor.” Interestingly, sometimes, they go on trips together, alone, to the state dacha. Also, he’s officially the owner of the five Pusheenian state cats. One of them is named “Dribnytsi.” Nikolai IV has a much younger, non-disowned cousin (Alexis) who is the current Tsarevich, and the latter has his own children.

Economy: State capitalist with state-led industrialization, with rapid development (sponsored by massive foreign investment) and rising living standards. Powerful military-industrial complex that massively outstrips the consumer sector. Decently strong petroleum sector. Extremely strong agricultural sector (earning it the name “Breadbasket of Nova Terra”). Built for rapid mobilisation in the case of war, much like the Commonwealth of Britannia-Ireland (Petro Kramar even described it as an inspiration for Pusheen Country’s current economic policies). Significantly demographically damaged by the Great War, and corruption and poverty are somewhat of an issue (especially in rural regions). Some forced labour camps (“correctional camps”) exist, and are some of the worst on Nova Terra in terms of conditions. They’re also used to produce some goods, which are marketed as such (perhaps unsurprisingly, there’s not much of a market for them outside Pusheen Country). Major agricultural reforms in the 1890s have significantly increased the tax base and have made the economy a bit more sustainable, though farmers generally remain relatively poor. Many people have also been shipped out to colonize the northern regions of the country, and towns of tens of thousands have sprung up to accommodate oil refineries, factories and other such things. The Great Northern Railway is an economic project to develop the railways of the country, connecting the historically dominant south to the northern periphery, and to connect the east to the west (with a Kannanese leg continuing from the Pusheenian border; a political controversy in Kannan).

Military: Strongest military of any neutral state. Has about 1,330,000 personnel in total (mostly in the Army). Primarily defensive doctrine designed to exhaust enemies (like Rapusia, which is more responsive to public opinion) politically and economically. Principally an army-focused nation, with the Air Force primarily existing as CAS and the Navy being an afterthought with a flagship (the Slava) being a “lightly used” battleship bought second-hand from the Commonwealth as lend-lease during the Great War. Service rifle: M31, a semi-automatic battle rifle in 7.62x54mmR.

Culture: Orthodox Tankism is the dominant religion. Cats are a national symbol even in a Tankist context, since they reflect the nature of Pusheenia as a country (stubborn and independent). Flag: horizontal tricolour of black (vigilance), white (unity), red (sacrifice). Conservative, mildly anti-intellectual and nationalistic in general, but many people aren’t (in fact, there is a thriving socialist tradition in Pusheen Country, even if it’s often surveilled and repressed by the Security Service). Historically seen as a backwards nation due to its use of serfs until the 1860s. Language: Pusheenian.  

**Note on the Pusheenian space program, the Ministry of Space:** Essentially titular, with progress being made on launching a satellite into space by 1966.

**Heads:** Tsar Nikolai IV; Minister-President Petro Kramar (essentially a rubber stamp for the regime, dog person).

### Empire of Dai-Koku
Pop. ~138M | GDP C139bn | Per capita C1,010 | Gini 43 | Territory ~594k km² | Capital: Shinkyo | Currency: Dai-Kokuese yen

Rapidly industrializing power, export-oriented and possibly (not certainly) overextended. Constitutional monarchy under Emperor Norihito (Banwa Era; r.1952 - present), quietly influential - if he’s in a cabinet meeting, everyone knows to shut up when he coughs. Friendly relations with Rapusia (and a Commonwealth before the early 20th century, now there exists something of a rivalry between them); fervent rivals with Ishanis since time immemorial (despite using the same writing system in part). Solism is the primary religion (supported by the state, with the Emperor in particular being revered), with Eastern Tankism as a secondary religion. The interservice rivalry between the Army and Navy spills over into the National Diet (Rikken Seiyukai & Rikken Minshuto); where any actual ideology is usually secondary to the actual topic of debate: namely, which branch of the military should get the most funding, and whose strategy should be followed? To elaborate, the Army’s Inland Plan focuses on an internal military and economic buildup that develops Dai-Koku’s existing sphere of influence, and the Navy’s Rimland Plan focuses on containing Ishanis via building economic influence in its neighbours. Proto-cyberpunk (neon, grand architecture, skyscrapers, combined with astonishingly small nooks and crannies and alleys) aesthetic in Shinkyo, the capital; the largest city in Nova Terra by population along with Tank City (having surpassed it in population in only 1962). Politically independent from all major factions as a major founding nation of the Neutrality Pact, and is thus strongly committed to its own sovereignty (and quite frankly not much else, hence why you can often find them supplying arms to both sides of any given conflict) and the sovereignty of others, so long as they don’t interfere with Dai-Koku’s own economic activity. A surprisingly mountainous country on the Dai-Kokuese archipelago (including Nanto), with a largely temperate climate (with the north being cold and snowy).

Economy: Zaibatsu system (crony capitalism), where in an ostensibly free-market economy, large businesses profit from a close economic relationship with the government, which uses this influence to support policy goals. Economically imperialist. Large economic sphere of influence including Kannan & Horonyia; in latter exploitation of natural resources; trades with most countries. The Dai-Kokuese yen is pegged at 360 yen to the crown, though some experts suggest that the yen might be severely undervalued, and it remains a periodic political issue in the UTP even as Dai-Koku continues to defend it. Extremely loose monetary policy, designed to reduce the costs of exports. Economic miracle since the 1950s (with regular GDP growth rates exceeding 10-11%). A leader in robotics, electronics in general, increasingly automation, and weird vaguely cyberpunk things. Rather than using automation to replace humans, they use automation on top of humans, so humans do the same amount of work to produce even more things, creating a middle class that’s getting richer and richer and larger and larger. (the wonders of industrialisation!) Possesses the Special Administrative Regions of Nanto and Choron, with pro-free trade laws different from those of the mainland, where the zaibatsu hold great sway, while the keiretsu remain confined to the mainland for the time being.

**Big Four zaibatsu:** The Big Four are the four most important megacorporations that dominate the Dai-Kokuese economy, comprising Mitsuo (the largest until 1961, when it collapsed triggering the **Economic Wars**, where smaller keiretsu attempt to exploit the collapse to expand their market share; a corporate cannibalisation of one of their own, with it generally being done and dusted by Q4 1962), Shigeo, Ryuza and Horitoshi. All of them indulge in various industries and so don’t really have any particular specialization (though Horitoshi is known for robots).

Military: The Imperial Dai-Kokuese Armed Forces value quality over quantity, due to postwar restrictions stipulated by the victorious Grand Alliance in the aftermath of the Great War; with no more than 300,000 combat personnel total permitted. Dai-Koku has adhered to a much higher extent than Rapusia; with the current active-duty personnel count of the Dai-Kokuese Armed Forces being 289,000. Generally high morale due strict, but fair and ultimately reasonable superiors (in deliberate contrast to the indoctrination and fanaticism of the Great War era), along with very good training and discipline. No single Air Force, with the Army controlling shorter-range fighters and tactical bombers, and the Navy (4th or 5th-largest in the world depending on the year) controlling strategic bombers and their own fighters (especially for their carrier wing, universally recognised as one of the best of Nova Terra). Uses the Type 9, an assault rifle that uses a highly experimental 5.8x42mmR cartridge.

Culture: Strong culture of responsibility, saving face and conformity, inherited from Ishanis and the values of the Faith of the Eight; which the corporations, crucially, did not create. Simple-minded wisdom is valued as part of the Dai-Kokuese Spirit; “that which defines Dai-Koku.” Also a bit of a growing soft power, with Dai-Kokuese cultural exports like anime beginning to reach as far-flung as Columba and North Hydra. Car brands in Dai-Koku usually have multiple different brands of dealerships, unlike other nations (where if you need, say, a Royal Duchess, you just go to the Royal dealership and get one). The Dai-Kokuese flag has a white field with a red circle in the middle, with 8 red rays coming from it and becoming bigger and bigger until they reach the edge.

**Note on the Dai-Kokuese space program, the Dai-Kokuese Space Agency (DKSA):** Only just getting off the ground, with a satellite launched in 1962 (with the sponsoring of Horitoshi).

**Interesting cultural parallel between Dai-Koku and the Commonwealth:** Dai-Koku and the Commonwealth have very different ideas about what honour is (face/reputation vs. duty-fulfilment respectively) produce similar practical expressions of determination in combat, and thus genuine mutual respect (including allowing surrendering Commonwealth soldiers the honours of war; in contrast to their horrendous treatment of POWs; even in a world where POWs are regularly used as cheap labour). Mamane Nomura, a noted Dai-Kokuese author who wrote numerous books on the warrior spirit, called the Commonwealth a “samurai country.” 

**Head of government:** Hiroshi Kaga, a reformist of the Rikken Minshuto that nevertheless primarily wishes to lessen the impact of corporate mistakes on the common population, rather than outright fighting these corporations, major engines of the economy.

### Federation of Diplomatians
Pop. ~62M | GDP C122bn | Per capita C1,967 | Gini 30 | Territory ~8.4M km² | Capital: Ambessa | Currency: Diplomatian pound (also known as the starbuck)

Constitutional monarchy (Oswald V) and parliamentary democracy (unlike the Commonwealth, though, it’s constrained by a Constitution and a Charter of Rights), introduced after the 1864 Diplomatian revolution that ended absolute monarchy and provoked the beginning of the decline of the long-powerful aristocracy. A federation of states, with its name coming from the fact that it was formed principally by diplomacy and outright purchasing territories (unlike many other countries on Nova Terra at the same time), though conflict was involved (like the Eureka Revolt of 1616, an early attempt to wrest control from the aristocracy that controlled the country). Under a long-standing SDP dominance since 1889 and the New Economic Policy (a radical, social-democratic economic policy that changed Nova Terran economic thought), which has remained relatively stable to the present day and will continue for the foreseeable future with presumably only minor interruptions. Aligned FNA member; but prefers soft power, diplomacy, and plausible deniability over force. Rivals Horonyia and Dai-Koku for Southeast Antares and Cetus influence. Although universally recognised as a Great Power, before the Great War, it was generally seen as an oddity; an eccentric nation in the bottom-right corner in the map influencing tiny islands that nobody cared about. At least, until it joined the Grand Alliance, won the Great War, and gained a permanent seat at the Community of Nations Great Council. The state religion is Olympianism, but the state is secular in practice; with the monarch in particular having to select a patron god (the current being Meerais, god of the sea). Parliament is dominated by the Social-Democratic Party, which has ruled almost continuously since 1889 and their platform of the New Economic Policy, though the Liberal-Agrarians do oppose them. Close alliance with the Commonwealth (both are island monarchies and are actually linguistically compatible; Diplomatian remains more archaic). The temperate coasts are separated from the desertic inland (called the Nullarbor) by mountains, with the especially prominent range that follows the east coast that traps rain on the east coast being called the Diplomatian Range. Well known for its murderous wildlife, including the infamous drop bears.

Economy: Social market economy with a high-trust society and a highly developed welfare state; which some have attributed to Diplomatian culture and its values of precision and responsibility. A major exporter of beef, mutton, wool and textiles, with gigantic cattle stations dotting the land. A major industrial power too, with a strong automotive industry. Fairly typical growth rates for a Nova Terran country, in contrast to the outright booms elsewhere; a bit of a sore spot in Diplomatian politics. However, some argue that this is because Diplomatians has completed its process of industrialisation and is a relatively evenly developed country and so expecting more growth than that is delusion.

**Airships:** Not decorative, but cost-effective, normal transport and military assets integrated into ATC with separate lanes and schedules like any other aircraft. Diplomatian Airways (also a standard airline that uses heavier-than-air craft) is the only operator of international passenger airship flights on Nova Terra. The Diplomatian Federal Armed Forces and DASRI operate many airships as well, for reconnaissance, logistics and atmospheric research.

**Grammatical quirk:** When referring to a group of people of Diplomatian nationality, “Diplomatians” is plural, but when referring to the country specifically, “Diplomatians” is singular. The average Diplomatian will be quick to correct you if you make a mistake in this regard.

Military: Diplomatians has highly capable air force (C.110 Phantome jets imported from the UTP), a competent navy and extremely, absurdly advanced aerospace research. Technology has been described as brilliant, overengineered and occasionally alarming (one columnist described the pace of their advances as “a race with themselves to make manmade horrors within comprehension”); like their construction of a directed-energy weapon designed to shoot down enemy ICBMs and aircraft, officially titled Project Excalibur, but colloquially called the Diplomatian Death Ray. Uses the Model 10, a select-fire battle rifle in 7.5x55mm. A generally well-rounded, professional military (the Diplomatian Federal Armed Forces; with 780,000 active-duty combat personnel) exists (if a bit underfunded due to the lack of war), with most funding going to the Navy (6th-largest in the world) and Air Force. 

Culture: Well known for its murderous wildlife, including the infamous drop bears. Also a major exporter of watches and chocolate; with both being major cultural icons, The flag is sky blue with four stars argent on it in a cross pattern. The design has also made it onto the reverse of every Diplomatian coin, earning the Diplomatian pound its more famous nickname: the starbuck.

**Note on the Diplomatian space program:** It exists principally as a side project of the Diplomatian Air and Space Research Institute (DASRI), with a satellite being launched in 1958 and no ambitions for the moon for the time being.
 
**Head of government:** PM Charles Ebbott, an idealistic social democrat, belonging to the left wing of the SDP.

### Empire of Ishanis
Pop. ~549M | GDP C117bn | Per capita C214 | Gini 53 | Territory ~12.2M km² | Capital: Arlsrest | Currency: Ishanian yuan

Semi-feudal corporate empire. The Ishaan Dynasty has been ruling since 987 NE; one of the longest continuous reigns of a single dynasty on Nova Terra. While technically foreign (being a Yukien clan), most Ishanian people accept them due to their general assimilation into traditional Ishanian culture. The constitutional monarch is currently a 14-year-old child, Zaitian (whose predecessor, the adult Fulin, went missing in 1951). The legislature is the unicameral Legislative Yuan, a body that is partly appointed and partly elected by essentially only the top 1% of Ishanian society due to wealth requirements for voting. The Faith of the Eight is dominant and endorsed by the government. Language: Ishanian (many mutually unintelligible dialects; represented using Traditional Chinese script; it’s probably disingenuous to say that it’s a single language). Historically led the Greater Ishani Empire at its height, extending far into what is now eastern Rapusia. Four successor states came to be after a partition plan after the death of the Second Emperor: Great Ishanis (survived; you’re looking at it now), Great Zadash (now part of Canausbel), Red Horde (now part of Pusheen Country) and the Khalashvid Empire (dismantled from the inside by Rapusic lords, obviously part of Rapusia now). Fervent rivals with Dai-Koku, and exercises some degree of influence in the region (including in Kannan) as the self-proclaimed Middle Kingdom. However, it’s not a particularly prescient priority to the leadership, which is more concerned with court intrigues and/or internal economic development. Generally socially conservative. Tridemism (as represented by the Society of Ishanian Nationalists and various affiliated paramilitaries) is a growing political force that supports Han Ishanian nationalism and a unique form of Ishanian democracy focused on nationalism and welfare; which is currently fighting an insurgency against the Imperial government since 1961 (the Ishanian Crisis). The second-largest country on Nova Terra in land area, and the most populous. Member of the Taunfurt Pact.

**Interesting warlords:** The Pandong Clique is easily the most famous warlord state, which controls the southern port city of Panyu and the surrounding province. It is ruled by General Lo Yang with a deeply opportunistic fist, having some of the most liberal trade laws in Ishanis. The Red Banner Army of Ulanka is a major warlord state that has adopted a variety of socialism while still (at least nominally) swearing fealty to the Emperor in Arlsrest. The Jin clique controls Datong province, and controls a significant portion of Ishanis’ coal reserves, becoming filthy rich due to this. As well, the Principality of the Mountains is located in Central Ishanis, and is one of the most impoverished regions of the country (and that’s saying something). It has been described as a barrack-state, where most things exist to serve the Prince and his armies.

Economy: Western financial institutions introduced by Rapusian advisors in the late 19th century have been syncretised with local patronage systems into a pseudo-feudal structure (feudal corporatism). Ishanis principally exports raw materials, agricultural products (like rice and grain) and cheap machinery (like arms and vehicles). Inequality is primarily fuelled by an extremely rich elite of magnates, some of the lucky warlords and aristocrats; if you somehow took the top 0.005-0.5% or so of earners in Ishanis out of the equation, the inequality would be more comparable to that of the UTP. Triads and hongs are significant problems in urban areas (though local authorities tend to tolerate them and use them against dissidents until the central government comes knocking).

Military: The Army is composed of Imperial Army (generally Rapusian-trained troops from the provinces directly under Imperial administration; composing about 68% of the active-duty combat personnel) and various warlord and regional armies with more diverse quality and training. Primarily focused on quantity over quality, constantly pressuring enemies until they buckle. Outdated, but still sizable Air Force (exclusively used for close air support against insurgents); in contrast, the Navy is effectively an afterthought (with a flagship, the INS Xiangyun, being a battleship that would’ve been somewhat outdated during the Great War). Uses the Gewehr 99, a straight-pull bolt-action rifle chambered in 7.92x57mm that was once the service rifle of Rapusia during the Great War.

Culture: Ishanis has a long and storied history, which is reflected in its traditional culture (which has influenced the culture of all of its neighbours, for good and for ill). Based on the values of the Faith of the Eight Virtues, it values filial piety, propriety, a respect for elders and compassion towards the people around you and the world. Despite this, it’s often used to repress people who want a higher standing in life. Ishanis is also a major producer of silk, produced the old-fashioned way (using silkworms, brought over from Terra). The flag of Ishanis possesses a red field, with a white cross and a blue first quarter, with a white sun with twelve small rays that don’t extend to the edges (which serves as the Imperial Sun, the symbol of the monarchy, which is often cut out in protests against the regime).

**Head of government:** **Chief Minister Hsiang Juo-Kang** is an effective, but ruthless ruler; a competent economic administrator and brutal dictator managing Nationalist (Tridemist) insurgency in the south and disloyal warlords. Profoundly cynical at the personal level, but still something of a patriot. Resigned peacefully after the first democratic elections in Ishanis, and lived a quiet life without ever answering for his atrocities, due to the fact that many Ishanians saw him as a national hero.

### Auxurian Republic
Pop. ~88M | GDP C95bn | Per capita C1,085 | Gini 56 | Territory ~8.9M km² | Capital: Tzantium | Currency: Auxurian denarius

Oligarchic federal semi-presidential republic established 1889 via military coup. The elites who led the coup still effectively run things 74 years later via managed democracy never designed for genuine power distribution. The ancient philosopher Tacticus wrote the *Usus et Theoria Belli Stultorum* (~4th century NE, during the Auxurian Peace where Auxur dominated the world), Nova Terra's primary military treatise; Tacticus denied its brilliance (describing it as an introductory guide for fools), his more sophisticated works were lost, and this introductory tutorial (with a disproportionate amount of wit in comparison with its reputation) became canonical. A tropical country, for the most part, but the southern heartland is relatively temperate. Largely an Orthodox Tankist country, though significant regions also maintain worship of the Twelve Olympians at the same time. Exerts significant influence in South Hydra, often clashing with Pangarap and trying to expand its economic pull upon the rest of the region as the Great Power responsible for the region.

Tzantium has walkable slums, a single metro line using 1910s–1920s rolling stock, and ALLCAPS serif station fonts as if the Empire never died (reflective of popular attitudes towards the Empire in its later stages as a reformist, democratizing and developing monarchy; with the last Emperor essentially just giving up in the face of having a female heir and the discontent of the landed gentry). Maintains a strong independence and neutrality streak, leaving it somewhat technologically outdated (in military terms, at least) by the 1960s.

Economy: Ostensibly capitalist, in practice dominated by the landed gentry of southern Auxur, supported by a boss system where a locally dominant oligarch known as a tribune dispenses favours in exchange for political support. Auxur is also a growing country (with rising living standards), with major exports in timber, seafood, minerals and some types of machinery. Industrializing, with rising living standards. There exists a notable population of peasants, which often support the system because of the benefits it indirectly benefits them. Technically only the tenth-largest economy in the world, behind Timernond.

Military: Genuinely competent military that has become somewhat radicalized and politicized by inequality. Brigades are traditionally known in the Auxurian Army as legions, and are numbered. Generally well-balanced, if conservative in tactical characteristics and doctrine, oriented towards the defensive side of things and not particularly well known for maneuver warfare. However, it remains somewhat outdated in terms of technology, as previously mentioned. The Auxurian Navy is respectable, being larger than the Timernondese Navy (in fact, overall it’s at 7th place in the naval rankings). The service rifle is the V.12, an indigenous semi-automatic rifle that fires an equally indigenous 7.62x45mm.

Culture: Based heavily off the Empire that preceded this, the Republic that followed it has remained relatively conservative in changing its culture. Auxurian, although the common language of the Auxurians, is often viewed as prestigious (being material for pretentious mottos), and many empires have sought to be a "Second Auxur" (including Rapusia). High culture is flourishing, while low culture increasingly diverges from it. Nostalgia for the Empire is a very common thing (even as it grows increasingly distant), even if most Auxurians think a restoration of the Empire would be a pipe dream. Auxur is also known for its excellent seafood. Although the majority of Auxurians are believers of Orthodox Tankism, a significant Olympianist minority exists in the northeast and the coasts. An enthusiastic football country, being a regular at the AIF World Cup, and characterized by a chaotic, improvisational style. The Auxurian flag is a simple horizontal tricolour of white, green and blue.

**Heads:** President Julian Crassus (oligarchic conservative aligned with the elites); PM Junius Brutus (progressive, working within a system designed to prevent genuine reform).

### Socialist Federal Republic of Timernond 
Pop. ~221M | GDP C114bn | Per capita C515 | Gini 34 | Territory ~3M km² | Capital: Blasengrad | Currency: Timernondese dinar

A socialist federation of five republics (and several autonomous regions) located on the Timernondese continent. A major rival of Ishanis, despite not being a Great Power (because of its underdeveloped power projection capabilities). An authoritarian (but popular) regime that delivers economic stability, development and a sense of common purpose under the leadership of President Marko Selanovic, a partisan hero during the Great War known for resisting Ishanis and fighting along the Canausbelites (their historical allies).

Formerly a unitary constitutional monarchy (the Empire of Timernond) from the unification of Timernond in 1818 to the end of the Great War (where in 1946 a referendum abolished the monarchy and replaced it with a socialist federation; before this the country was embroiled in a civil war between the pro-government coalition of socialists-monarchists supported by the Allies vs. national separatists that supported the Seven-Nation Army; lasted from 1941 to 1947). Close cultural ties with Canausbel, despite occasional ideological disputes.

Economy: Worker-directed (at least nominally), made of co-ops and corporations led by the state; the Socialised Economy of Bourquism. Possesses a developing heavy industrial sector and a very much present agricultural sector. Rapidly growing, but still dealing with post-Great War reconstruction.

Military: A largely-conscript based force of about 1,000,000. Largely pursues independent military development, though the Type 1 MT seems to be based on the T-58 of Canausbel. The service rifle is a Type 45 clone; the M58, chambered in 7.62x39mm.

Culture: A very diverse country with at least 12 separate ethnic groups. Even if they speak largely the same language, they still remain very attached to those identities. The flag of Timernond is a horizontal tricolour of blue, red and white, with a small red star with a golden border located at the hoist side of the top blue stripe.

**Selanovicism:** Selanovicism is a type of pragmatic authoritarian socialism first invented by Marko Selanovic. It supports the political repression of “bourgeois elements” while still allowing for a notable degree of personal freedom and socialist internationalism. It supports a sort of Timernondese pan-nationalism centred around Selanovic, the “Father of the Timernondese” and current dictator of Timernond (since 1944).

**Language note:** Timernondese was originally meant (in the 1840s) to be a lingua franca, meant to be the average of the major languages spoken in its territory. However, over the course of the years, it has accidentally become the first language of a significant majority of the Timernondese people, with the local languages being in danger of extinction. However, the Timernondese still feel very strong attachment towards their own state within the federation.

**Technology:** The technology of early 1960s Nova Terra generally resembles *our* late 1960s (use the rule of thumb that in any given post-Great War year, Nova Terra is about 4-6 years ahead). Air travel is becoming increasingly accessible, and the ranges of the aircraft used have increased after the embracement of jet power. Dieselization (or gazolisation, as it’s called on Nova Terra; with the name of what would be diesel here being gazol or pedan fuel) and electrification of railways is well underway, and Dai-Koku in particular became the envy of the world in 1957 by building the world’s first exclusively high-speed rail line, leaving many other countries seeking to compete. Nuclear energy harnessed for peaceful purposes is a major interest of several nations, and renewable energy (primarily hydroelectricity) has been researched and even been used to great effect (like in the UTP, where the majority of the electricity of Borea comes from hydroelectric dams). Smaller, more fuel-efficient cars (like the Daisan Coronet, one of the most popular cars in the entire world) are generally the norm around the world. Metallurgy is at its peak, and steel has many different varieties used for many different purposes. The periodic table has been filled from 1 to 102 (the Earth names of the natural elements will be used for simplicity’s sake except in the case that it wouldn’t make sense; the artificial elements may have their own names). However, intentional anachronisms and minor technological divergences exist, such as the Diplomatian Death Ray or the development of Pepper’s Ghost illusions that look like holograms in Dai-Koku (in fact, they’re a major attraction of Shinkyo, which is famous for them). **Computing:** Cybernetics; which is to say, circular causal processes often leading to computer integration and adoption of digital systems for the distribution of information; is a current cultural and scientific fascination across Nova Terra. Computers exist, but are usually large mainframes; like ICC’s Z1: the first computer powered by integrated circuits (first sold for business, government, military and educational clients in 1959 for the low price of C133,000+), widely adopted (and copied) across Nova Terra for such utilities as planning industrial production and simulating physics. powered by increasingly-advanced programmable chips. Factories in richer parts of the world (like Dai-Koku) are beginning to supplement their human workers with automated tools, now using programs instead of delay circuits. Semiconductors are already important enough that Dai-Koku and the Commonwealth (through their corporations; respectively Horitoshi and Halifax Semiconductor) have a rivalry over who can make the better integrated circuits and chips. 

**Major corporations:** Many corporations exist on Nova Terra, from defense contractors (like Commonwealth Engineering of the Commonwealth) to technology companies (like the International Cybernetics Company of the UTP; formerly known as simply International Machinery Company) to food producers (like the United Fruit Combine of Rapusia; doing business as Alford) and everything in between. Many of these corporations are supported by the Great Powers they come from (even if officially they’re publicly traded), regularly reaching dizzying heights only possible with this state-business collusion. The socialist world is largely (but technically) excluded from this due to the fact that their private sectors are largely made of cooperatives like Red Star of Canausbel (a chain of convenience stores), though, if they were, they would be strong competitors. The state-owned Canneft, after all, is one of the most important producers of oil on Nova Terra, along with the private Commonwealth-Canausbel Petroleum Corporation (founded during the Great War, a joint venture between the two countries that has grown increasingly estranged after the Commonwealth discovered its own oil reserves), the sole exception to Canausbel’s ban on traditional private corporations (even if the Canausbelite government owns 40% of its shares).

**An important note on the corporatist influence of economies:** Most developed economies on Nova Terra generally have become something akin to corporatism, even if they’re officially free-market systems, socialist systems, or something else entirely - featuring major economic blocs (such as corporations and unions) negotiating with each other, with the government as either a party or a referee, and sometimes influenced by the other parties. Negotiation between these parties remains an important part of these structures at their commanding heights, even more important than competition. There is a relative lack of mobility in terms of enterprises, with small to medium-sized businesses usually remaining small to medium-sized, and large corporations generally remaining large corporations. There exist exceptions, of course, but that is to be expected with any rule. Ambitious people usually have to work within whatever system they’re in, rather than disrupting it in the typical sense. One such manifestation of this tendency of “postliberalism” is neo-liberalism (not to be confused with neoliberalism), the postwar liberal consensus of the FNA. Neo-liberalism on Nova Terra is the sort of managerial capitalism that has emerged after the Great War, that preserved the democratic governance, private property and rule of law of liberalism, but rejected the laissez-faire capitalism that had failed to prepare these liberal countries for the Great War. Some nations adhere to this orthodoxy rigidly, others are loose about it, and some go even further beyond it; but they are all influenced by it.

**Inequality note:** The Great War affected everyone greatly, even the neutral countries (Kingerse, the peak of neutrality called it the Emergency; and experienced rationing and a cost-of-living crisis). The generally free-trade and (relatively) globalised status quo of the pre-Great War days gave way to protectionism in the Great Powers fighting the war. The consequences of this were catastrophic for many neutral countries, many of whom relied upon the advanced machinery and capital exports of these Great Powers. Androsia in particular was hit especially hard because of its political and economic structure, and has never recovered.

**Tariff note:** The average tariff rate on Nova Terra is about 15-18%. Some countries, like the Commonwealth (20%) and Rapusia (28%) have higher average rates, while others, like the UTP (9.6%) remain more supportive of free trade.

**Great Recession (1979-1983):** Triggered by a release of some of Canausbel’s oil stockpiles in the face of a long rally in oil prices (peaking at C60.73 in December 1978, with worries in FNA nations about a cost-of-living crisis). However, set up by a long period of oil speculation with the decoupling of physical oil production from the exchange of oil futures (“paper oil”), after Canausbel cut oil exports in 1973 in response to a persistent decline in oil prices over the past couple of decades. There was an expectation of investors (including banks and governments) that oil prices would continue rising; betting on a relatively constant (and low) amount of oil exportation; which was broken when Canausbel announced a release in oil stockpiles to take effect in January 1979, thus leading to a mass panic-selling of these barrels of “paper oil,” leaving investors exposed, and causing a credit crunch by the evisceration of collateral (the paper oil; thought to be a stable, appreciating asset). Dai-Koku was initially shielded by geographic distance (and a general preference for investments in real estate), then struck by the concurrent bursting of a speculative property bubble in 1981. Deflation was a massive problem for Canausbel and Dai-Koku; though it was relatively universal and managed to force the UTP off the gold standard by June 1979. Produced a Liberal Party landslide in Diplomatians 1982 (ending ~century of SDP governance) and a SDPR victory in Rapusia 1983 (détente, slow decolonisation, piecemeal economic reforms).

**Railways:** Railways are one of the most important methods of transportation on Nova Terra due to their incredible efficiency (a locomotive, after all, almost always can haul several times its weight). The standard rail gauge on Nova Terra (also known as Rapusian gauge) is 1473mm; enough for a cart or a small Living Tank to fit through the appropriate loading gauges, though many nations use different gauges within their own borders; like Commonwealth/Canausbelite  gauge (1435mm), Skiyian gauge (1668mm), Pusheenian gauge (1524mm) or Cartegenian/Dai-Kokuese gauge (1067mm). As a consequence (and perhaps enhanced by Nova Terra’s general impulse to put anything even remotely important under the responsibility of the state), railways are often put (at least in great part) under a state rail carrier. Here are some of them:
- United Tank Provinces: In the United Tank Provinces, most railways east of the Hydran Mountains (the Rockies; in Earth terms) are under the ownership of the Société National de Chemins de Fer (SNCF). The west remains dominated by private companies, though the SNCF does run freight trains into “their turf.” However, the state passenger carrier, Intercity (originally a brand of the SNCF, but then spun off), absorbed the vast majority of passenger rail operations in the country in 1974 (including those of the private companies). Some of their most iconic rolling stock are the Metroliners (named in Britannian because it makes them sound cooler; high-speed EMUs with the aerodynamics of a slightly angular brick), Highliners (bilevel coaches; relatively rare on Nova Terra, used in the western regions); the F40PH (iconic) and the TGV (nuclear-powered; finished in 1981 as part of the UTP’s efforts to prevent a deflation death spiral). The East and West Coast Trunk Lines are electrified (along with several minor commuter and tunnel lines), and progress is being made on the electrification of the Tank City-Ventôse line.
- Rapusia: The railways of Rapusia are owned entirely by the Rapusische Reichsbahn (RRG). Although much of central Rapusia’s railways are now electrified, towards the east, most of the trains run on gazol or even steam. The RRG was also affected severely by austerity measures under the Bechenplan, which cut rural service by 40% and necessitated the closure of hundreds of stations in order to “cut costs.” The RRG is the operator of the Trans-Rapusia Express, one of Columba’s most impressive passenger trains. Rapusian trains, despite their reputation for punctuality, remain at best only on time about 75% of the time (when the rest of the developed world is at like 85-90% in terms of punctuality).
- Canausbel: Canausbelite National Railway (CNR) is a state-led cooperative that runs all of Canausbel’s railways. Due to an unusual degree of Commonwealth assistance in the construction of their railways, they use Commonwealth gauge (which has proven to be handy in the case of invasion like during the Great War). In fact, quite a bit of their rolling stock is imported from the Commonwealth! They also run the Oriental Express, a luxury service (appointed by Marshall cars; like Pullman cars but Nova Terran) which runs from east to west and back again.
- Commonwealth: Commonwealth Railways (rebranded to Commonwealth Rail in 1965, with its iconic logo, “the worm”) (CR) is the state rail company of the Commonwealth of Britannia-Ireland. However, its Britannian and Irish operations are effectively two separate railways under a single branding system (and later electronic classification system; TOPS). CR greatly benefitted from the government’s make-work and reconstruction programs, with significant expansion and improvements during the 1950s and 60s. Gazolisation is proving to be a bit of a difficult task (due to noticeable public opposition). The West Coast Main Line has become fully electrified as of 1962.
- Pusheen Country: The Ministry of Railways directly runs the railways of Pusheen Country. It handles its own leg of the Great North Express (running from Pusheenigrad to St-Magdalen in Kannan, with a switch in locomotives at the border). The Great Northern Railway (the northernmost railway in the world) is operated by them.
- Dai-Koku: In Dai-Koku, most railways are owned by Dai-Koku Rail (DKR), though unusually there exist many private commuter carriers. Although their high-speed lines are in standard gauge, most lines are in Cartegenian gauge (1067mm). Around major cities like Shinkyo, through-running between rapid transit and the commuter lines connecting them is extremely common; a concept that is very difficult for foreigners to understand. Overwork is unfortunately still a problem for DKR drivers, and has led to tragedies like the Kinhama derailment of 1960 (where a section of the line without ATP had a commuter train, whose driver briefly dozed off from exhaustion while overspeeding, and hit the back of a train of oil tankers, leading to 264 deaths).
- Diplomatians: Railways in Diplomatians are run by the Diplomatian Federal Railways (DFR). The DFR is run, according to foreign observers like the Times, like ruthless clockwork. 
Nova Terra has invented push-pull operation, multiple units and many other railway technologies.

**A note on the Conmunity of Nations System of Accounts:** The Community of Nations System of Accounts is a set of measurements used in order to compare countries in matters of economics. The two main measures are Gross Domestic Product (value added per annum by the residents of a country) and Gross National Income (despite the name, the total income earned by a country’s citizens everywhere). GDP and GNP statistics generally exclude dependent territories, territories that are not officially considered part of their sovereign nation (e.g. colonies), and they are often divided by the metropolitan population in order to obtain per capita figures for quick comparisons of individual prosperity. Many nations also use nonstandard units of account like the socialist Net Material Product (a measure of the production of the agricultural and industrial sectors, excluding services) and median household income (used in the UTP as a measure of relative prosperity). The Community of Nations has also standardised a Human Development Index based on the dimensions of life expectancy, mean years of education, total expected years of education, literacy rate and GNP per capita; which is used to guide reconstruction but is not part of the System of Accounts.

## MILITARY

**Mechanical tanks** evolved from imitation of Living Tanks. Emerged from experiments in the late 19th and early 20th centuries with small, crewed armoured vehicles, before the Great War massively accelerated their development. Post-Great War tactical experience produced the Multipurpose Tank (MT), achieving the mythical balance of armour, speed, and firepower. In contrast, the pre-MT Commonwealth Tamworth cruiser tank prioritised speed and combined it with light armour; and the Livingston infantry tank prioritised durability and traction over all else.

**Military technology in general:** Most countries on Nova Terra generally use bolt-action or semi-automatic rifles left over from the Great War era. Even when the rifles have automatic capabilities, they usually are chambered for full-sized rounds, thus being *battle rifles*. However, some Great Powers, like Rapusia, Canausbel, the Commonwealth and Dai-Koku have begun to adopt assault rifles during the later parts of the Great War and after its end. A lot of support equipment (like LMGs and artillery) has also survived from the Great War.  Camouflage is growing increasingly popular; primarily used in specialist contexts, with most nations preferring solid colours for most uses because it’s expensive. Missiles are already seeing some notable use in many of the world’s militaries in all branches, though notably in cruise missiles, ATGMs, AAMs and anti-ship missiles. Guided munitions in general (including laser-guided bombs) are growing in popularity, with the UTP once again being an innovator in this regard with the ASM-2 (the world’s first fire-and-forget, radar-guided glide bomb, 1944) and the BG-212 (the world’s first laser-guided bomb, 1962), and Rapusia setting up the concept in their increasing desperation towards the end of the Great War (with the GX, a radio-guided remote-control bomb first used in an anti-shipping role in 1942). However, anti-aircraft guns (including those from the Great War, like the iconic Tator 40mm autocannons) continue to be used, but are growing less and less relevant as aircrafts’ operational altitudes and speed increase. Specifically in the navies, guns on ships have shrunken (to calibres of around 3-5 inches) and have become capable of burst fire like autocannons. However, many navies retain ships from the Great War era, from when most ships were oriented around breech-loaded guns. This includes the few countries that continue to possess traditional battlecruisers and battleships. A significant portion of the world’s aircraft carriers are also hulls from the Great War, but in the UTP’s case, they’ve often been modernised enough that they barely feel vintage aside from their size (see the Marat-class aircraft carriers, built during the Great War and now serving as ASW-oriented carriers). Drones have become quite advanced, being able to follow pre-programmed routes and holding patterns before delivering munitions, while delivering a shoddy television feed to the pilot far far away; or directly flying into the enemy with an explosive payload in a suicide attack. However, purpose-built drones are usually used for reconnaissance or target practice; true UCAVs remain in an experimental stage. They’re already proving their utility in service with the United Provinces Air Force, with San Martino recently having been hit with multiple suicide drone attacks from the UTP in 1962, and munitions-carrying drones (the MQ.100, a sort of reusable remote light bomber, if you will) based on long-ranged variants of pre-existing drones are being deployed across some of the Great Powers. Living Tanks, due to their upkeep costs, have been relegated to specialist roles in counter-insurgency, reconnaissance and other support responsibilities. 

**Living Tank doctrine:** Living Tanks in their military heyday (comprising much of world history before the Great War) were tactically used as a mix of shock cavalry and mobile command strongpoints (when ridden directly by their handlers). They were frequently ridden desant by infantry for extra mobility, guided by their handlers to go where the combat was most apparent. As well, commanders and standard bearers would sometimes ride on them desant, and with the support of the handler, would help guide their regiments in the right direction. They could also function as fire support, and given the right circumstances, could charge en masse like cavalry at full speed, cannons blasting, although this would be a rare event; immortalized in the annals of war. Handlers themselves are a respected (if not common) specialisation in many Nova Terran militaries, and are caretakers, veterinarians and tactical partners all at once; adapting to their assigned Tank’s habits and needs while achieving tactical objectives with the support of the Tank. The Tank can act independently and adapt to accomplish these goals set out by the handler via commands; though it is ideal that they stay close by to monitor and support the Tank, and also keep it on task. The assignments are themselves decided by bureaucrats who try to match characteristics between prospective handlers and Tanks, who typically go through a brief bonding period where compatibility is evaluated for the final decision. Once, a Tankian Army handler spent 7 years before he was finally assigned a Tank!

**Notable doctrines:**
- UTP: The UTP's military is built around the doctrine of Full-Spectrum Superiority, that extends the battlespace to include industrial, political and morale factors. Aims for total superiority in these areas, along with overwhelming dominance in the battlefield itself, in order to achieve strategic goals. NCO initiative on a tactical level, which doesn’t necessarily translate to a grand-strategic level. Heavy usage of artillery, missiles, and other, precise long-ranged weaponry in order to reduce casualties on the Tankian end, destroy enemy morale and weaken the enemy’s resistance. Early air superiority is a crucial component of this.
- Rapusia: Fast, decisive, and mechanised campaigns prioritising transport routes and logistical nodes as targets in order to disrupt organised resistance. Mobilisation and deployment plans are essential, and campaigns are planned out in broad strokes with approximate timetables to prevent overextension. Local officers are encouraged, however, to adapt in service to the plan due to the inherent unpredictability of war. Inefficiencies begin to accumulate usually about a year into the campaign, though they only do so at a linear rate, as they pile onto each other as the returns for Rapusia diminish. However, it is pretty easy for a state facing Rapusia to either collapse after the initial Rapusian offensive, or expend so many resources just surviving it in the first place that defeat in a proper war of attrition is inevitable. Fully mechanised post-Great War.
- Canausbel: Operational Depth: let enemies overextend themselves in Canausbel’s mountains and deserts; with stay-behind units constantly harassing them, then when the time is right, counterattack; applying home-field advantage to destroy and/or capture enemy units. Air forces exist to provide air support, attack the enemy’s rear and to prevent the enemy from using their own CAS (air superiority), and naval forces are used to secure supply lines or hamper the enemy’s.
- Commonwealth: Principally a naval power that concentrates on keeping trade routes open and preventing blockade-running (and is also very skilled at night-fighting), though land doctrine is accounted for; focusing on attrition warfare; supported by a disproportionate industrial base that thanks to dirigisme can be mobilized rapidly for total war, immense stubbornness that exhausts the enemy, stay-behind units that make occupation a living hell, tactical flexibility and NCO initiative. Second-highest morale of any Great-Power military, behind only Dai-Koku. On a tactical level (universally, actually), values an overwhelming, continuous and constant volume of fire.
- Pusheen Country: Strong fortifications combined with large amounts of artillery support reduce the amount of manpower necessary to hold off enemies. Highly defensive (“turtling”), though not incapable of maneuver warfare. Heavy use of guerrilla warfare via stay-behind units in case defensive lines are breached. Favours immediate close air support.
- Dai-Koku: General qualitative superiority (in morale, training and in some areas technology) due to postwar military restrictions on manpower; namely, a maximum of 300,000 service members and a permanent ban on conscription. However, the interservice rivalry between the Army and Navy hampers coordination on a strategic level. Dai-Kokuese soldiers are fanatically loyal to their officers because these officers are trained to be strict, but ultimately fair. Each branch maintains their own air wings, with the Navy having purview over long-range aircraft and the such and the Army having purview over shorter-range aircraft and air defense. 

**Note on the conduct of war on Nova Terra:** Nova Terra *does* have an international convention on the conduct of war; the Lundburgh Conventions, I through IV, superseding previous national laws of the signatories on the conduct of their respective armed forces (such as the Military Conduct Acts of 1714 and 1890 of the Commonwealth); that govern the conduct of war: namely, it prohibits the targeting of civilians, the targeting of medics or medical facilities, the targeting of wounded or shipwrecked people and the use of torture for any purpose. Anything not covered here is technically legal, including the use of POWs for labour and weapons of mass destruction, though there exist nuclear non-proliferation treaties. Some states, like Timernond, openly possess (6) nuclear weapons while not being part of the treaty, while Qurkia and Dai-Koku neither confirm or deny the existence of their nuclear arsenals. As well, many countries on Nova Terra, including the Great Powers, maintain (in secret or openly) maintain stockpiles of chemical and biological weapons; like the Commonwealth’s stockpile of Defense Weapons (which include horrific nerve agents like C4H10FO2P). However, the actual strategic use thereof greatly stigmatized, and if anyone actually does use them, they would quite possibly be formally condemned by almost everyone and sanctioned so hard their descendants will still be impoverished in a century; if not just bombed and invaded by a Great Power or two. Nova Terrans themselves say that merely making a law won’t keep people from using horrific things in war; people violated the Lundburgh Conventions during the Great War anyway, despite their existence; and that it has to be ingrained in society to keep these things from happening again. Hence, why the Lundburgh Conventions haven’t been updated since their adoption in 1907.

**Military peculiarities:** Rapusia invented the goose-step in the mid-18th century, and it has since spread to places like Ishanis and Diplomatians. ## CULTURE AND SOCIETY

**Languages (represented by translation convention, used for etymologies, linguistic relationships and translations and the such; in the same way Tolkien did it):** Tankian (Quebec French), Rapusian (High German), Britannian (British English), Diplomatian (Old English), Canausbelite (Russian), Dai-Kokuese (Japanese), Pusheenian (Ukrainian), Auxurian (vulgar Latin), Ishanian (Standard Chinese), Skiyian (Spanish), Horonyian (Hindi), Kannanese (Czech), Central Lander (Dutch), Sharisam (Farsi), Qurkian (Egyptian), Timernondese (Serbo-Croatian), North Contanan (Swedish), Konnelian (Finnish), Kingersean (Greenlandic), Pangarapese (Filipino), Dupelander (Amhara), Atlantean (Greek), Trarorian (Portuguese), Cavalistani (Tamazight), Fillerantic (Romansh), Ataraxian (Coptic), Syuddese (Vietnamese), Flipadossan (Romanian), Rafari (Farsi), Eram (Swahili), Varengi (Azerbaijani), Kekish (Georgian), Jesusese (Hungarian), Erinese (Irish), Otterese (Burmese), Mayaran (Malay), Suleymani Qurkian (Emirati Arabic), Undestanindestrean (Mongolian), Linnian (Dzongkha), Interretean (American English, spoken in Twittertopia, Tumblria and Reddit Island).

**Nationalism and its origins:** Nationalism, perhaps inherited from Terra, has been around on Nova Terra since the very beginning of human colonisation. It may have ebbed and flowed depending on the events, but the general trend (aside from outliers like the immediate pre-Great War period) was that it got stronger over time at a slow but constant pace, with it only recently beginning to decline (or is it just stagnating? Maybe it’s just returning to the regression line) with the aftermath of the Great War. Most countries on Nova Terra (even relatively progressive ones like Diplomatians and Kingerse) tend to be ethnostates: states designed for one ethnic group. Royal intermarriages were relatively rare, and civilizational states like ancient Auxur or, more recently, Rapusia, tended to be the exception (and Auxur today is very clearly a nation-state, just a prestigious and old one). The UTP started as a federation of states which were part of a single overall ethnogeographic identity (“Tankian”). Even until the end of the Great War, multinational corporations were rare and notable because of their multinationality; and even then, they were still clearly tied to their home country. The far-right in many of the Great Powers is often seen as a bunch of radical, perhaps violent weirdos; not necessarily the epitome of evil.

**A follow-up on systemic xenophobia:** Universal and a systemic issue (metawise), distributed in degrees across the world. Using the Commonwealth as a baseline; UTP and Diplomatians are somewhat less; Canausbel is roughly equal; Rapusia is somewhat higher and Pangarap is much higher. Foreigners and non-citizens are explicitly legally treated as having less rights than citizens across the vast majority of Nova Terra’s countries, and are often surveilled or segregated into specific areas when in other countries, and deportation is frequently possible for offenses that would merit a fine for citizens. In the books of the Commonwealth, a typical example, a conviction for disorderly conduct and many other crimes can lead to deportation if the convicted is a non-citizen. Economic migrants and refugees (especially refugees), however, are in general more tolerated than expatriates; though this is relative to a really low baseline that still discriminates against them. The UTP is relatively progressive in this regard; non-citizens are allowed to travel to most areas of the UTP, and naturalisation is actually a feasible idea rather than a pipe dream. Trade unions are notably opposed to immigration in most Nova Terran nations, seeing it as a means for employers to depress wages and lay off workers; while business often ends up relatively supportive of immigration (if only because it lets them obtain labour on the cheap).

- Tankie: A Tankian (often jocular).
- Phal: A man in Fillerant. Unfortunately, many segments of the population of Fillerant see no issue with its use.

**A note on militarism:** Militarism and military-industrial complexes are common things on Nova Terra. Countries regularly spend 6-7% of their GDP on their military (the UTP is actually viewed as a slightly undersized military power because they only spend about 5% of their GDP on it), and this militarism is often viewed very highly as a necessity to deter enemy aggression and potentially a Second Great War (in view of the fact that the Seven-Nation Army only unconditionally surrendered after the use of terrifying force). However, pacifist movements exist, and also have grown to be surprisingly popular in the richer countries of the world in the generation which has been born after the Great War (Generation Alpha; the first generation to actually be given a name rather than just assigned one ad hoc based on when they were born).

Even the (relatively) cosmopolitan UTP generally looks down on Columba (and Rapusia in particular) even while letting Columban immigrants in. **Legal systems:** Most countries on Nova Terra follow varying mixes of Auxurian-based civil law (where law and rights are codified in broadly applicable documents) with customary legal systems throughout. It’s also often associated with the inquisitorial system, where courts directly investigate. The Commonwealth and its cultural sphere is unique because it has adopted a different legal system from everyone else; a system where legal precedents form the great majority of the country’s law, and rights are “discovered” through empirical reasoning rather than written down in one convenient document (dubbed “common law”, or simply “Commonwealth customary law”). The legal profession also varies depending on the system. Auxurian legal systems have a wide variety of jurists that study the specific legal codes of the country they practice in, like notaries. In comparison, the Commonwealth has major types of jurist: attorneys (which represent the client in courts) and solicitors (which handle things outside the court; these are much more common). The Rapusian model is based on the Auxurian model, but introduces a separate commercial code; and has proven to be influential in its own right in places like Ishanis, Monte Bianco and Dai-Koku. Canausbelite law is a mixture of Auxurian law, Canausbelite customary law and socialist law, and has spread where socialism has spread (just adapted to local circumstances).

**Human rights:** There exists no single document governing human rights on Nova Terra. Enforcement of these rights is often based on a piecemeal, subjective point of view that depends on how the country views such rights in the first place. The Community of Nations’ Declaration of Human Dignity, though, is the closest thing anyone has to an articulation of what a human would have in an ideal world from a Nova Terran perspective: in general terms, freedom from arbitrary repression by their own government. This high standard, however, is frequently violated by the nations of Nova Terra, typically provoking strongly-worded open letters signed by noted activists, NGOs and public figures.

**Women's rights:** General Nova Terran position (using Rapusia as example): abortion is broadly illegal except in cases of danger to the mother’s health and after rape, with a strong homemaker expectation and a general tolerance for women in non-traditional roles like engineering and science. Not cartoonishly oppressive; not progressive. However, progress is being made in these areas in the more progressive countries of the world, with abortion becoming an issue of note in the UTP in particular during the late 1950s.

**LGBTQ+ rights:** A general stance of apathy exists on Nova Terra towards most sexual minorities. Lesbianism has generally been non-criminal and ignored for most of Nova Terra’s countries across history, and gayness is generally something that falls under a idea called “don’t ask, don’t tell,” where public expression is frowned upon but private expression is a relative non-issue (except in the case of pedarasty, once common in Atlantis, Auxur and Androsia, now viewed with disgust). Transgender (including nonbinary) people remain viewed with suspicion in most places, though in the UTP (and the UTP only) you can change your gender as registered on your passport. Your sex cannot be legally changed except through exceptional means, though; and people who actually make the physical transition (though surgery) are often viewed as curiosities. 

**Forced labour and slavery:** Many Nova Terran nations (including the UTP) regularly use prisoners as free manpower for industry, construction and the such, with prisons often being… not exactly the most comfortable of places to be, including the state-owned ones. However, outright chattel slavery was slowly criminalized over the course of the 19th century, with only a few minor stragglers left that still legally allow it (like Qurkia, Rafaristan and Suleymania). Debt slavery remains a problem in the developing world.

**The Pride Flag:** In 1968, the remains of what could only be a Terran spaceship were excavated in a backwater part of Rapusia. On it, was a drawer, featuring a smooth but oblong object with a vaguely hemispherical base (a dildo, presumably; but you can’t be too sure in archaeology like that), and the tatters of a flag with six horizontal stripes: red, orange, yellow, green and blue. Due to the great protests of that year (especially those demanding recognition of the contributions of homosexuals and the like), the gay community of Nova Terra adopted that flag as a symbol of themselves.

**Art and other cultural traditions:** Nova Terra, like our world, produces many things of exclusively cultural value. Due to the Great War, modern art has become increasingly abstract, often rejecting the traditional norms of beauty of prewar art; being dubbed avant-garde as a result. One such movement is Futurism, which idolizes imagined futures and progress towards them. As well, orchestral works have grown to be increasingly experimental, building upon their Classical and Romantic (passionate, expressive) forebearers; and pop music and its cousins are beginning to dominate the minds of young people. Many nations also possess unique traditions of their own. For instance, in many Columban countries, there is a traditional break taken around 2:00-4:00, often lasting for an hour. In the Commonwealth, this is known as “tea time”; in the cultural sphere of Skiyia, as a “siesta” (you often take a nap here). As well, many countries possess forms of traditional culture that don't fall into any of these categories, like Kingersean drum dances. Film and TV has also joined this wave of experimentality, and the studios of the UTP have been joined by many competitors from places as far as Dai-Koku and the Commonwealth. Fashion, too, has adapted with the times, with popular, casual clothing becoming less and less formal and more and more willing to expose skin (though diplomatic and formal uniforms remain generally fairly similar to those of the 19th century). Dai-Koku in particular is beginning to produce some peculiar comic books in their own somewhat cartoony style, and adapts them into animated serials of generally a more fantastical nature compared to the serials of other nations. And this doesn’t even begin to cover the idiosyncrasies of Nova Terra’s militaries…

**Literature:** Nova Terra has a long tradition of literature. Long before the Great War, epic poems and plays were written, like the the Eight Ishanian Classics (including the Romance of the Warring Kingdoms and the Dream of the Red Chamber) and the works of Al Smith (the greatest playwright of his age). However, unlike our Earth, it has remained relatively common to serialise novels chapter-by-chapter in literary magazines, like modern radio serials. Nova Terra also has a thriving pulp fiction and comic market; sensational and lurid tales of horror and romance (and sometimes genuinely brilliant writing!), often consigned to relative obscurity unless their authors break out of this niche. Science fiction and fantasy (influenced by Futurism and modernism) have made a resurgence during the Great War and beyond as people increasingly seek to find refuge in fiction. 
Some of the greatest novels in Nova Terran history include: 
- The Romance of the Warring Kingdoms (1450s): An epic tale of the unification of Ishanis in the first century BNE between the three states of Wei, Lin and Shan. Yes, the gap between the events described and the writing of this is very much real. It was primarily intended as a guide to morality for Ishanian emperors, but still holds up reasonably well as an entertaining novel (if still a bit didactic; half the fun comes from ridiculous schemes falling apart).
- Don Futilo (1601): The first modern novel; written by Almirante Cervera, which tells the story of Don Futilo, a Skiyian nobleman attempting to futilely imitate a false version of a noble, chivalrous past. Some say that it is an allegory for a struggle against conformism. Others say (with much greater accuracy) that it is a warning against glorifying the past to an excessive degree.
- Travels of a Man Across Nova Terra (1713-1715): A satirical travelogue centred around one misanthropic man’s travels to fantastical locations across Nova Terra that reveal flaws in his very own society.
- Arrogance & Audacity (1813): A novel by Commonwealth novelist Anne Mordaunt, about a girl known for matchmaking finding love in the places she didn’t expect.
- Far West (1831): An extremely long epic novel about the Tankian frontier by Antoine Rochon. Follows the story of two families (one good, one evil) and their conflicts throughout the decades, permeated throughout with an idealistic commentary on Tankian imperialism (and that maybe the locals do have a point, even if they have weird beliefs and try to raid the two families’ homesteads).
- A Tale of Two Countries (1838): Louis Morton’s most famous work, a satire of the First Canausbelite Revolution from a liberal perspective; showing off the perspectives of two closely tied families: one from Lundburgh and one from Medyeva.
- Low Expectations (1841): Also written by the legendary Commonwealth novelist Louis Morton, it is a satire of aristocratic life from the perspective of the hapless commoner adopted child of a nobleman, and the comic shenanigans that follow (and eventually, how that adopted commoner child finds love).
- Land of War (1868): A Canausbelite novel by Vladimir Chernozow, with a fictional narrative centred around the Canausbelite Revolutionary Wars interspersed with Chernozow’s own commentary on history and the like.
- The Sisters Karenina (1883): A Canausbelite novel by Artur Rokovsovsky, about two sisters who become very interested in politics on the eve of the Second Canausbelite Revolution (and their descent).
- The Thomas Mercure Mysteries (1890-1916): A series of mystery novels by Marcel Marchand of the UTP about a great detective (written from the POV of his assistant) and the various cases he has to face throughout his life.
- Tankia (1927): A novel by Pierre Poincaré that attempts to capture “the spirit of Tankia” inside one great novel in six volumes; following a man’s journey across the country in search of work, and maybe finding out that there’s something more important than reaching the end.
- Tension (1931): Written during the Intrawar period in the Commonwealth, John Parker uses a stream-of-consciousness, fragmented style to communicate the feeling of great unease and anxiety permeating life in the Commonwealth at the time.
- The Hole-man (1935): A children’s novel by Henry Rolf Ronald Davis (a Contanan immigrant to the Commonwealth; thus a patriotic Orthodox Tankist Rapusian-Commonwealthman), about a Hole-man’s journey There and Back Again. Surprisingly good, even for adult audiences, due to Davis’ knowledge of the many myths of Nova Terra.
- Memories of the Great War (1949): A semi-autobiographical novel by Karl Gustav Schaefer, describing the experience of futility and despair of a typical Rapusian conscript towards the final years of the Great War. Some critics saw the novel as excessively bleak and nihilistic (with an iconic quote literally going “it was a war without reason”). Other Rapusian veterans saw it as perfectly accurate.  

And some of the most famous novelists on Nova Terra include:
- Li Tenghui (1391-1483?): The principal compiler and editor of the historical texts that would become the Romance of the Warring Kingdoms.
- Anne Mordaunt (1775-1831): A prolific and brilliant romantic Britannian writer, well known for her slightly mentally unstable, brooding, but cute male protagonists (Mordauntic is the term now).
- Louis Morton (1797-1867): A Commonwealth novelist who used his books to satirize the issues of the Commonwealth and industrial society. As a whole.
- Antoine Rochon (1803-1883): A fanatical Tankian patriot and idealistic progressive (for his time). Viewed as one of the greatest writers in Tankian history, if not of all time.
- Vladimir Chernozov (1828-1873): A Canausbelite writer of political and epic novels. Very interested in the psychology of his characters.
- Artur Rokovsovsky (1831-1883): The inventor of the philosophy of Volya; derived from Tankist ethics to posit that doing good is worth it even if it’s hard or futile.
- Pierre Poincare (1900-1961): Born in Sierrana, a prolific Tankian writer on very definitely Tankian subjects. A socialist, funnily enough.

**Surveillance states & the police:** Many countries on Nova Terra (even the democratic ones) possess surveillance states (or rather, extensive security and surveillance apparatuses). They are used for many things, from police investigations of actual criminals to gathering dirt on political dissidents and foreigners; and are often managed by various agencies. These surveillance states frequently function without the consent of the people being surveilled, and are very often hidden. However, how much the government acts on information gleaned from this surveillance varies, and in many of the more liberal states a local may know this and not worry at all (and be relatively right to do so judging purely by legal consequences). Police brutality is also, unfortunately, a relatively frequent occurrence in most Nova Terran countries, often being deployed against protestors of every single ideology under the sun, so long as they oppose the state on some serious issue. Most democratic countries tend to be relatively moderate (like Kingerse, where this is essentially unheard of), but even then incidents of police violence still occur and are publicized every so often (like the beating of Michael Moseley, a far-right anti-immigration activist present at the Haymarket immigration riots in 1961 in the Commonwealth; it was notable because most bobbies in the Commonwealth don’t have firearms, only having batons).

**Demographic note:** The Great War skewed the male:female ratio in favour of females in Columba (including Canausbel and Pusheen Country, both transcontinental countries that extended into Antares, and in Canausbel’s particular case, into Libra as well), causing a notable, even severe imbalance in the demographics of Columba that may last to the end of the 20th century. At this point Britannia-Ireland (islands), Fillerant (neutral), Kingerse (neutral, islands) and North Contana (neutral) might be the only countries in Columba that have something resembling a normal gender ratio. However, in Britannia-Ireland, the general postwar baby boom has had a notably weakened effect, and the gender ratio still favours women (52-48).

**Sports:**
- *International Games:* Large international competitions in a wide variety of sports and disciplines every four years (summer and winter), first edition in 1900 (to celebrate the new century), but cancelled for much of the Great War only to return with a vengeance in 1952. Notable sports include athletics, aquatics, gymnastics, the decathlon and Lorician sports.
- *AIF World Cup:* football (soccer) world championship, dominated by Columba and Antares. The first edition was held in 1950, but due to football’s widespread popularity, it is already one of the biggest sports events in Nova Terra. The latest edition, held in 1962, had Skiyia win the Cup.
- *Tankian Gridiron League:* All-Stars Game championship (UTP); the Grey Bowl is the Canausbelite equivalent (and the older one, since Canausbel invented the sport in the first place).
- *St-Étienne 500:* A stock car race held at the start of the Hydran Motorsport Association’s season; a quintessentially Tankian sport known for crashes that miraculously leave everyone alive.
- *Type 1:* The ultimate league for open-wheel, single-seat type racing cars. Possesses two competitions: the Drivers’ and the Constructors’.
- *The Grudge:* A Commonwealth-Diplomatians Test cricket series held annually from 1898.
- *Boreal Cup:* club ice hockey (not on a national level, that would be the International Games); major nations include the UTP, Kingerse, Commonwealth, North Contana, Pusheen Country, Kannan and WQ.
- *Lorician sports:* Sports involving the use of Living Tanks, from dressage to racing. There are many events for these (primarily catering to an aristocratic audience) like the Royal Epsom, with the Tanks used there having extremely long pedigrees.

**Tourism:** Tourism on Nova Terra has become increasingly accessible over the span of the post-Great War years, and especially the 1960s. However, international tourism is still principally seen as an upper-middle class to upper-class activity due to the various bureaucratic hurdles you have to overcome to even get a visa in the first place (including the possibility that the application is rejected outright for no reason). Some notable tourist destinations include the beaches of south Skiyia and Reddit Island, the resort towns of western Canausbel, the ski resorts of Monte Bianco, Skiyia, Fillerant and Rapusia, and the UTP’s… everything, really. 

**Education:** Education on Nova Terra is frequently reflective of the priorities of the nation that funds it (since most Nova Terran nations have public schools, in the sense of publicly-funded educational facilities). Since the real interesting differences start after elementary school (after all, you can’t really incorporate indoctrination into addition; though trust me when I say there are great inequalities in educational quality across the world), I will skip there. In most Nova Terran societies, secondary education also includes some ideological education, though in democracies it remains relatively subtle. For instance, in Rapusia, the civics course teaches the political system of the Empire and demonstrates it to be a normal system that functions rather than the stereotype of it being a feudal relic. Boarding schools also exist, though these are rare and generally confined to sufficiently large countries or countries descended from the Commonwealth and its own system of government schools, “public schools” (that still have tuition; just open to all who can afford it) and private schools (which have entrance exams).  
In Nova Terra, tertiary institutions exist as well, like colleges and universities. Research universities do very much exist, and periodically cooperate with foreign universities on certain important projects (like the University of Lozar’s collaboration with the University of Wellsborough on a particle accelerator; the former is in the UTP and the latter in the Commonwealth). Universities tend to be hotbeds of radical political thought, and of course partying, drinking and all of that. Still, universities are perfectly willing to throw you out if your grades slip (or your grades just aren’t that good to begin with), so be careful!  
Here are some of Nova Terra’s top universities:

**Newspapers:** There exist many newspapers on Nova Terra. Together with newsreels and the emerging television broadcast, they are Nova Terrans’ principal sources on current affairs and cultural shenaniganry. Some are very reliable, and others are considered gossipy tabloids (like the Commonwealth’s Daily Astre), and still others are considered mouthpieces for whoever’s printing them. Here are some of them, by importance:  
- The Tank City Times (quite possibly the world’s most trusted newspaper)   
- The Economic Chronicle (the principal finance newspaper of Nova Terra, a Tankian publication)  
- The Times (of Lundburgh, an equally respectable newspaper, and the probable namesake of most newspapers that have “Times” in their masthead)

**The rise of environmentalism:** Tankism, the dominant religion of the planet, already places Living Tanks above humans. As a result, most nations have facilities and territories where Living Tanks wild, domestic and retired can roam freely in a natural environment; termed sanctuaries collectively. As a result, nature reserves (land left purposefully underdeveloped to attempt to preserve the natural environment) as a concept emerged in the 1870s in the UTP, preserving the creation of the Great Tank as a public space with free entry and exit, which has led to a surprisingly strong conservation movement as of the mid-20th century.

**Alcohol:** Nova Terra is one of those worlds where nobody agrees what is the best way to get drunk with the boys and then wake up the next morning in a completely different room and with no idea what you did last night. Here are some of them.  
**Beer** is an alcoholic beverage made by brewing and fermenting starches from cereal grain - usually malted barley. It is one of the most popular hard drinks on Nova Terra due to how common grain is across the planet. Beer in regions where it is popular (like central Rapusia and Canausbel) is also associated with various social traditions, like pub games.  
**Wine** is an alcoholic drink made from fermented juices, native to the warmer, coastal regions of the world like Monte Bianco, the east coast of Auxur, the west coast of the UTP and southern Skiyia, where grapes and their varieties can grow well. Wine in these regions is a very social drink, with tasting in particular being a common activity, with its own terminology to assess the quality of said wine. It’s also collected and stored in dedicated wine cellars, either as an illiquid asset (wine is a liquid, this does not reflect its nature as a financial asset) or as a method to improve its taste with age.  
**Mead** is an alcoholic beverage made from fermented honey, with an alcoholic content ranging from 3% ABV to more than 20% ABV. It originates from ancient Auxur, which was one of the first adopters of apiculture, the agricultural practices pertaining to beekeeping. Mead usually doesn’t keep the sweetness of honey thanks to the fermentation process, but sweeter varieties do exist, and are particularly common along the coast of Kirbistan. Today, it is most often consumed in Hydra, especially in the former colonies of the Skiyian Empire such as Western Quaristanicaval and New Skiyia, where it has developed into a modern industry.  
**Spirits** (also known as liquor) are a large category of drinks made by the distillation of already-fermented grains, fruits, vegetables and sugar. Due to the distillation process inherent to their creation, spirits have a higher alcohol by volume than other drinks, which has led to the, being dubbed hard liquor in some regions of the world. Some examples include, in no particular order:
- vodka, a drink made of distilled liquid from fermented cereal grains and potatoes, a specialty of eastern Rapusia and Pusheen Country.
- rum, a sugar-based drink originating in the tropical, sugar-producing regions of Konnelia, Mayara and Horonyia, but since spread elsewhere by maritime trade.
- gin, a drink made of fermented grains flavoured with juniper berries, common in far-western Rapusia.
- whisky, a liquor made of fermented grain mash, often aged in wooden casks, a specialty of Britannia-Ireland.
- brandy, made by distilling wine, usually consumed after dinner, something very common in the United Tank Provinces and to an extent all regions where wine is made.
- tequila, a very unique distilled beverage made from the blue agave plant, native to one (1) specific region of New Skiyia and since popularized across New Skiyia.

**Political vocabulary:** Nova Terra has a wide variety of political terms with very different meanings than they have on Earth, often replacing such concepts that never really took off here (like communism and fascism). 
- Indigenism: A far-right ultranationalist and palingenetic ideology that supports the preservation and promotion of “indigenous culture” and the suppression of “foreign” and “cosmopolitan” concepts.
- Maximalism (aka Duyverism): A type of revolutionary technocratic socialism where power is concentrated in a Directory of intellectuals and experts in order to replace bourgeois structures.
- Frolovism: A type of revolutionary socialism that advocates a constant revolution and mass mobilization, combined with expansionism.
- Neosocialism: An authoritarian corporatist ideology that supports radical nationalism and a “national revolution” led by a technocratic elite. 
- Postliberalism: Liberalism’s responses to the failures of laissez-faire capitalism. Also used to refer to “neo-liberalism,” a managerial-capitalist ideology that supports cultural and political liberalism while remaining economically statist and interventionist.

**Diplomatic protocol:** Great powers exchange embassies (led by ambassadors) amongst each other. All other nations generally exchange legations (led by envoys extraordinary and ministers plenipotentiary, or just “ministers”). Ambassadors outrank ministers at functions, and being an ambassador is generally more prestigious than being a minister, because ambassadors personally represent the head of state of a Great Power. Due to the principle of diplomatic reciprocity, only Great Powers send ambassadors amongst each other, while Great Powers send and receive between themselves and non-Great Powers (no matter how close the relationship is). Kingerse uniquely hosts and sends embassies despite not being a Great Power, being informally accorded this status for its role as a mediator.

**Heraldric symbology:** Crosses, due to the lack of Christianity in a conventional sense, symbolize humanity (due to how common they were as symbols on the flags of the forgotten Terran nations). In the UTP’s case, it represents the “spread of liberty to in all four cardinal directions.”

**Vexillological notes:** Common flag mistakes include standardising all flags to 2:3 (the Commonwealth Ensign is 3:5; Canausbel and Pusheen Country are 1:2); using Rapusia's double-headed eagle rather than the correct single-headed eagle on gold and using the incorrect colour shades (of course). Don’t take it too seriously though; most Nova Terrans won’t care unless you screw up big-time.

**Tankpox:** Tankpox is a dangerous viral disease that uses Living Tanks as transmission vectors while leaving them unharmed. Symptoms include coughing, nausea, fever and eventually a rash that turns into fluid-filled blisters that, even after the infection goes away and they turn into scabs that fall off, leave scars. It was the first major disease to have a vaccine (first used in 1797, invented by John Crow of the Commonwealth), and over the course of the past couple of centuries, outcomes have improved, though mortality rates remain high in developing nations (overall, mortality rates are between 8-30%, with the likelihood of being disabled by it being somewhat higher). Eradication efforts, sponsored by the Community of Nations, are ongoing, beginning by disinfecting Living Tanks and population monitoring. Eradicating it in humans remains a long way away.

**Interesting wildlife:** About 20-40% of Nova Terra’s animal species (e.g. dogs, chickens, cats, cows, sheep, spiders, horses, etc.) seemingly have no relation to any of Nova Terra’s native wildlife; and can thus be deduced to be imported from Terra. In the Great Ocean, there can be found “mosasaurs” (not actually mosasaurs), large vaguely reptilian creatures that breathe air but swim around and eat animals like fish. There also exist Leviathans, great, snake-like deep-sea monsters that have been historically stereotyped as horrifying sea monsters… before it was found in the 1870s that they primarily eat small fish and usually don’t try to hunt humans. In 1959, a species of plankton that could feed off radiation was discovered in a basin in the Great Ocean, off the west coast of the UTP. How this works remains a subject of dispute.

**Flora:** About 10-20% of Nova Terra’s plant life and fungi have no Nova Terran ancestors, and can again be deduced to be from Terra (like sugar maples, oak, wheat, barley, mulberries, apples, roses, etc; presumably they were staple crops or otherwise liked); perhaps due to terraforming on the part of the early colonists. Some of the most distinctive trees on Nova Terra include ringwoods (vaguely birch-like trees known for firm but flexible wood), marshons (trees with unusually blue leaves and brittle wood) and toorabs (gnarled trees known for producing savoury fruit).

**Cryptids and mysteries:** Nova Terra has plenty of mysteries presumably unrelated to geopolitics. For instance, in northern Ishanis, we can find a set of tall, black monoliths with no clear reason for their existence. Notably, they have presumably been here since long before the population of this region, let alone the human population of the planet. There also is the tale of the Skiyian destroyer Burgundi, which once completely disappeared off the face of Nova Terra for a month while on a routine deployment off the southeast coast of the UTP (a region already known for mysterious disappearances; even if it’s also one of the busiest shipping lanes in the world where numerous ships pass by without any trouble) in June 1956… before turning up on the west coast of Skiyia, aground, remarkably intact aside from having run aground, with all of her crew missing as if they vanished without a trace. She reentered service the next year after a refit. As well, in 1927, a battalion of Ishanian soldiers once disappeared for a week while heading to a deployment in Konnelia. When they were found again by a search party… they were all dead, and often mangled horribly. Mysterious track marks of what could only be some kind of animal never before seen were found, heading into the mountains. There also are legends of the Undercity, an entire subterranean world where the whole world is literally upside-down, with skyscrapers extending downwards like stalactites; home to untold riches stored there by ancient bandits. According to folklore, the city of El Dorado in Tejaho is built upon an entrance. Last of all, the previous Emperor of Ishanis (Fulin) was preparing for a state banquet in 1951, before opting to retire to his room for a bit. After several hours, he had not returned, and after searching the Forbidden City and its environs, he was declared dead in absentia, forcing his 2 year-old son Zaitian to become the Emperor (albeit under a regency).

## INTERNATIONAL CRISES (since the Great War)

**Rapusian Overseas War (1947-?):**
- Rapusia, even after the Great War, had massive colonies, especially in Libra
- As a consequence, Rapusia had to deal with persistent, low-level insurgency, which sometimes flared up into even larger uprisings like in Nyassaland ‘55
- These uprisings were frequently suppressed with brutality, though by the 1960s there was more oversight from civilian authorities in Brandenstadt
- Often, though, they signed off on the same brutal practices that the Wehrmacht did when they were unsupervised…

**Grease War (1948-1949):** Kekistan vs. Cavalistan (Cavalistani victory)
- First proxy war of the Cold War (Rapusia vs. Canausbel)
- Kekistan invaded Cavalistan for oil, justified it with something else (border dispute)
- Cavalistan pushed back, taking some of southwest Kekistan in the process
- National pride increase in Cavalistan (already high off victory in the Great War), instability in Kekistan that led to military coup
- One of the last truly conventional wars

**Anglo-Twittertopian War (1948-1959; kinetic phase 1958-1959):** Twittertopia vs. Commonwealth (Commonwealth victory)
- Persistent low-level naval shenanigans and denunciations by Twittertopia (seeking to annex the Commonwealth’s Hydran Sea colonies for their valuable resources), skirmishes galore (1948-58)
- In the final phase of the conflict (1958-59), Twittertopia directly attacked the territories they claimed, but was repulsed and placed under a blockade by the Commonwealth
- The blockade caused a humanitarian crisis, which the CoN partially succeeded in mitigating by sending aid and later mediating a ceasefire
- The Commonwealth won, but had a taint on its international reputation due to the humanitarian crisis caused, which in part led to the Twittertopian Civil War beginning later that year

**Trarori-Jesusland War (1950-1958):** Jesusland vs. Traroria (Trarorian victory)
- Started over a territorial dispute over the lands surrounding Lake Traror (controlled and claimed by Traroria)
- Porte Alegreya, Traroria’s capital, is located on the north coast of the lake, but has a sizeable Jesuslander population
- Jesusland invaded, which led to a protracted conflict reminiscent of the early Great War in trench warfare, but in rough terrain due to their relative military parity, and with the increasing use of child soldiers to fill in manpower shortages
- Both sides alleged the use of chemical weapons
- Jesusland was repulsed, and signed a peace treaty renouncing its claims on Trarorian territory, but became closer to Rapusia & the Taunfurt Pact as a result
- Skirmishes continue between them

**Mayaran Emergency (1952-1958):** Mayaran Socialist Party vs. Commonwealth & United Mayara (Commonwealth victory)
- The MSP launched an insurgency in Mayara in the wake of the Great war and the rise of the Third International
- In response, the Commonwealth launched a reasonably effective psychological warfare campaign, which succeeded due to the relative lack of support for socialism in the region
- Although the Emergency was formally declared over by 1958, the MSP’s insurgents often relocated to neighbouring Konnelia and continued to launch raids into Mayaran territory, with slowly decreasing intensity as interest was lost

**West Cavalistan Conflict (1954-1956):** Cavalistan vs. Skiyia (inconclusive)
- Cavalistan, in response to reports of discrimination against the Cavali population of West Cavalistan (under the rule of Skiyia), and also due to Cavalistani confidence in the wake of the Grease War, began a campaign of border raids against Skiyia
- This led to several tense stand-offs and skirmishes in the mountains and deserts of their border until Kingerse negotiated a ceasefire between them that resulted in minor border adjustments

**Nyassaland Uprising (1955-1956):**
- A colonial uprising in Rapusian Nyassaland
- Brutally suppressed using such methods as carpet-bombing and summary execution of alleged rebels
- International condemnation, but at the same time this was a Great Power which promoted sovereignty (and people ended up backing Rapusia anyway), so nothing happened in the meantime

**Gyattisland Bush War (1958-1976):** Cartegenia & Gyattisland vs. Rapusia & Mianmaur People’s Liberation Army (Rapusian victory through pro-Rapusian ceasefire)
- Fought over Gyattisland, a Cartegenian client state used as a buffer against the Rapusian colonies and a container for restive ethnicities (including the Mianmau)
- Guerrilla warfare was present along with conventional warfare between Cartegenian-supported forces and Rapusia-supported forces
- Some people in the Rapusian colonies seized the opportunity to flee to Cartegenia, while others tried to flee to Central Land (with mixed results)
- Ended with a CoN-mediated ceasefire that made Rapusia guarantee Gyattisland’s independence while Gyattisland remained ostensibly sovereign, but still quite unstable and essentially one bad sneeze away from collapsing into a patchwork of ethnic states

**Twittertopian Civil War (1959-?):**
- Fought between Twittertopia, the National Salvation Government, and numerous other factions
- The NSG uses guerrilla warfare; insurgency continues
- Commonwealth & Reddit Island support the NSG (the former because it is pro-stability; the latter because it is anti-Twittertopia)
- The Twittertopian government, more specifically the Peace Force, establishes corporate freeports and a drug industry to fuel the war effort
- Some natives seize the opportunity to rise up and proclaim the Republic of San Miguel (named after the former name of Twitter City, the capital), headquartered in the resort town of Montaklar
- Community of Nations peacekeepers engage in humanitarian work
- Refugee crisis affects Maralgorange, Flipadossa, Kirbistan, and Auxur
- Some allege that the Great Syndicate is an extension of Maralgorange’s crime networks, even if Maralgorange is officially neutral in the conflict

**San Martino Insurgency (1961-):** San Martino & Pangarap vs. New Skiyia & UTP
- The oppressed, resentful indigenous peoples of the region of San Martino launched an uprising inspired by the ideals of indigenism (an ideology that supports suppressing all “foreign” and “non-indigenous” peoples and influences)
- The UTP, worried by such developments, began an intervention in 1962 via a couple of well-placed drone strikes in San Martino itself without any declaration of war or the such until several hours afterwards

**Second Ataraxi-Dupelander War (1961-1968):** Ataraxia vs. Dupeland (status quo bellum ante)
- Fought over water rights in the region
- Dupeland is fighting for survival (but is a theocratic absolute monarchy) and Ataraxia is a democracy (a corrupt, vaguely religious-socialist one with a weaker claim)
- Proxy war between Canausbel (Ataraxia) and Rapusia (Dupeland)
- War fought using Great War-era technology, primarily in the mountains
- Both sides attempted to use guerrilla warfare on each other, failing miserably and causing thousands of casualties
- Ended in a stalemate, mediated by the UTP, and even then minor skirmishes continue between them

**Ishanian Crisis (1961-1974):**
- Started after the Ishanian Army fired upon striking workers with artillery
- Escalated into a full-blown rebellion led principally by the pro-democracy (Tridemist) Society of Ishanian Nationalists, organised into the Blue Battalions
- In some Nationalist-controlled areas, a proto-state emerged known as the Ishanian Republic
- Many warlords tried to remain neutral, but frequently ended up crushed by the Imperials or overthrown from the bottom by the Nationalists
- Although the Nationalists lost in the end, Ishanis ended up adopting universal male suffrage for the Legislative Yuan in 1979, vindicating the Nationalists retroactively
- All in all, about 12 million Ishanians died because of the insurgency and its consequences
- In the first (relatively) free elections in Ishanian history, in 1979, a new party ended up winning in a landslide: The Society of Ishanian Nationalists
- This was a revival of the original Society rather than the exact same organisation, technically, but still
- To this day, many believe that if women were allowed to vote, the Nationalists would likely win by an even larger margin

**Syudd War (1964-1977):** People’s Democratic Liberation Front of Syudd vs. Commonwealth, Syudd (PDLF victory)
- Syudd was historically a Commonwealth protectorate since the 1880s
- Syudd gained independence in 1962
- Nationalist-socialist insurgency begins
- International criticism of the Commonwealth’s intervention
- The Syuddese clearly wanted the Commonwealth to leave already
- After a long asymmetric war, the Commonwealth withdrew in 1976, fueling a sense of bitterness within the Commonwealth
- War crimes committed by all sides (though particularly common amongst the rebels)
- Some spillover into neighbouring Rapusian colonies, which used the inspiration of the rebels to rise in open revolt against their colonial overlords

**Androsia War / Two-Day War (1968):** Rapusia, Silent Devils vs. Androsia (Rapusian victory)
- Rapusia sought to establish a land corridor to Qurkia via this territory (connecting the Libran colonies to another Taunfurt Pact member via Androsia and friendly Jesusland) and tie up loose ends regarding Androsia’s disloyal alignment with Rapusia in the 1950s
- The Androsian military was ill-prepared, and severe unrest among the underclass of beta males (largely farmers, workers, or soldiers) and women consumed most of the military’s attention
- Rapusia successfully curried favour for an intervention from the international community (and a mercenary company)
- After a swift campaign, fueled by overextension and encirclement of Androsian forces by internal unrest, a Rapusian puppet state was established
- The state was later renamed Elefthera in 1972 after a popular referendum

**Sharisam War (1971-1973):**
- Sharisam announced its intentions to terminate its observer status in the International while Sharisam’s population grew increasingly polarized over which side to take in the Cold War
- There was already an oppressed Timernondese minority in Sharisam, which Timernond used as an excuse to invade
- By the end of this short but bloody war, upwards of 300,000 had died
- Sharisam was left in turmoil, eventually leading to a socialist coup anyway

**Rapusian Invasion of Syudd (1980):**
- The PDLF had somehow managed to kill 30% of Syudd’s population through its own policies of forced agrarianisation and violent political repression in what became known as the Syuddese genocide
- Rapusia invaded after Syudd attacked Rapusian and Dupelander border outposts
- The war itself lasted only three weeks before the capital of Syudd, Valmari, fell to Rapusia
- The Republic of Syudd was established as a Rapusian puppet state under Pact-Commission Syudd
- The new government had to deal with mopping up remaining PDLF forces and rebuilding the country

### OTHER CRISES

**Scramble for Terra Antipolaris (1923-):**
- Began after the end of the first phase of the Great War, but went into high gear after the end of the Great War
- Primarily driven by Terra Antipolaris’ (presumed) untapped resources and the prestige of having people on the last southern frontier of Nova Terra
- The Commonwealth, Diplomatians, Western Quaristanicaval, the UTP, Rapusia, Canausbel, Dai-Koku, and Pangarap all established territories here, often with overlapping claims
- In the case of the Commonwealth and Western Quaristanicaval, this resulted in a minor territorial dispute in 1952 that was resolved peacefully due to the fact that it was essentially all due to a minor misunderstanding and unclear boundaries decided by drawing a straight line on a map without any coordination whatsoever with other nations and calling it a day

**Rafaristan Affair (1953):**
- The long-serving Prime Minister of Rafaristan, Mohammed Iravi, became the target of numerous protests calling for his resignation
- Protesters cited economic decline, election fraud, and Iravi’s perceived servitude to Qurkian interests
- The protests were suppressed violently by the Royal Police on Iravi’s orders
- The crisis reached its peak when the Queen of Rafaristan, advised by several known Tankian agents in her government, dissolved Parliament and called new elections
- The move was widely described as a de facto coup d’état by international observers
- Mohammed Iravi was placed under house arrest shortly afterwards by the military
- While under house arrest, UTP President Duranceau authorized an operation to kidnap Iravi and extradite him to the UTP on unrelated charges of drug trafficking
- The event shocked much of Nova Terra and sparked debate over what actions Great Powers were permitted to take
- The primary complaint among many Rafaris, however, was that they had been prevented from executing Iravi themselves; not that he had been kidnapped by the UTP themselves, they didn’t actually resent the UTP too much for it

**Rapusian Crisis (1956):**
- Caused by the rising cost of living in Rapusia, crumbling infrastructure, austerity measures, and widespread corruption
- Led by the banned Independent Social-Democratic Labour Party and the SDPR (with some tacit RDP support)
- Primarily involved strikes, walkouts (such as at the Wolecław arms works), sit-ins, protests, riots, and even one naval mutiny
- Some radicals attempted to occupy public spaces and government buildings, leading to their characterization as putschists
- Anarchy and chaos broke out in many cities during these riots, with looters and criminals exploiting the attempted revolution
- In the end movement was violently suppressed by the military with upwards to 4,000 killed
- However, the crisis ultimately resulted in increased parliamentary accountability for the Chancellor

**Stormy Sea Crisis (1956):**
- The Commonwealth accumulated massive national debt during its reconstruction efforts, much of it owed to North Contanan investors
- Grand Pensionary Jan Sterren decided that repayment had to be forced and threatened the Commonwealth
- This resulted in a tense military standoff, with the Commonwealth threatening nuclear retaliation if North Contana acted
- North Contana notably did not possess nuclear weapons at the time
- The UTP prevented escalation by threatening an embargo on North Contanan manganese exports
- Shortly afterwards, the Sterren government lost a constructive vote of no confidence
- A new Grand Pensionary took office and ordered North Contana’s forces to stand down
- The Stadshallare issued a formal apology and promised such a crisis would not happen again
- Rapusia did not intervene, as it was distracted by its own internal crisis, and much of the rest of the world was occupied watching the possible collapse of one of the world superpowers

**First Quaristanicaval Crisis (1959):**
- Pangarap threatened war with Western Quaristanicaval
- The Community of Nations responded with even harsher sanctions against Pangarap, despite the country already suffering from famine
- All major powers rejected Pangarap’s threats
- Pangarap backed down, but only due to pressure from the Great Powers, which its leadership openly resented

**Assassination of Franco Prat (1960):**
- Franco Prat was the newly elected Duke of Salmana and President of the Trade Confederacy
- The Trade Confederacy was strategically important due to the Salmana Canal, a major maritime route connecting the Central Ocean and Great Ocean
- Prat was a populist authoritarian who advocated for stronger Salmanese neutrality and national sovereignty over the Salmana Canal Zone
- He frequently kidnapped political opponents and had used violence against protestors
- He was assassinated in 1960 during a rally
- Officially, the assassin was a disgruntled intern
- Many outside the FNA believed the assassination was actually an OSIS operation intended to remove a potential enemy, with the intern acting as a proxy

**Highlands Incident (1960):**
- A Rapusian spy plane was shot down over the Scotian Highlands
- For three weeks, tense negotiations took place over the fate of the pilot
- The pilot was eventually returned to Rapusia
- He later published a memoir criticizing Rapusia’s handling of the incident

**First Skiyian Crisis (1961):**
- Partially caused by a coup d’état in Skiyia earlier that year
- Escalated dangerously when a stray weather balloon entered Rapusian airspace during a FNA military exercise simulating a conventional war with the Taunfurt Pact
- The balloon was shot down after being mistaken for a possible threat
- Before the truth was discovered, Rapusia mobilized forces and Skiyia responded with its own mobilization
- Cooler heads eventually prevailed and nuclear war was avoided

 Central Lander Refugee Crisis (1962):**
- An unusually large number of refugees fled Gyattisland across the sea to Central Land
- Many attempted the journey aboard heavily overcrowded ships due to the ongoing Bush War
- Central Land faced enormous diplomatic pressure to accept them
- Ultimately, approximately 60% of refugees were turned away by the Central Lander Navy
- Upwards of 17,000 people died during the crisis

**Cod War (1962-1968):**
- A series of fishing disputes between Auxur and Rapusian Libra
- Escalated into repeated naval confrontations
- Ended peacefully, eventually due to a lack of interest and Commonwealth mediation

**Koryos Missile Crisis (1966):**
- Koryos is a large Canausbelite island off its Annular coast
- It had been a demilitarized zone since Canausbel regained it from Rapusia in 1947
- Canausbel viewed the demilitarization agreement as a major humiliation
- Canausbel violated the agreement by stationing IRBMs on the island
- Canausbel argued that the restriction was illegitimate because it had been agreed to in a separate treaty from the Treaty of Tank City
- Rapusia responded by stationing ICBMs as close as possible to the Canausbelite border
- Canausbel followed suit
- Negotiations between Rapusia and Canausbel eventually prevented escalation

**Protests of 1968 (1968):**
- A wave of protests occurred across Nova Terra, especially in the UTP, Rapusia, and the Commonwealth
- Many protests escalated into riots
- Major issues included the Commonwealth’s involvement in Syudd, Rapusia’s colonial empire, events in Ishanis and the emerging gay acceptance movement
- At the time, many accepted that gay people existed but still viewed them as unusual or socially unacceptable
- Rapusian police raided a major gay bar in Brandenstadt earlier that year
- The counterculture movement also became visible for the first time in many FNA and Taunfurt Pact countries
- Environmentalists, leftists, nationalists, and anti-imperialists increasingly gathered at music festivals and similar events
- These gatherings brought ordinary citizens and counterculture groups into widespread contact for the first time
- The Free Love movement became the only major movement from this period to achieve many of its goals relatively quickly

**Second Kekistani Revolution (1968):**
- Kekistan became increasingly unstable due to declining oil prices and an economy dependent almost entirely on oil exports
- Conservative socialism became increasingly popular
- Socialist elements within the military launched a coup against Big Man Tyrone
- The new government aligned Kekistan with the Third International

**Second Quaristanicaval Crisis (1973):**
- Pangarap once again threatened war against Western Quaristanicaval
- Western Quaristanicaval decided not to rely entirely on international mediation this time
- It mobilized its military and threatened to seek Great Power support
- The crisis developed into a tense standoff
- International mediation eventually occurred despite Western Quaristanicaval’s objections

**Second Skiyian Crisis (1974):**
- The causes of the crisis remain somewhat uncertain
- One possible explanation involved a Skiyian soldier kicking a pebble across the Rapusian border
- Regardless of the cause, the crisis nearly escalated into another Great War
- The situation was only resolved because of the actions of one Tankian submarine captain who refused to launch ICBMs despite receiving orders to do so
- Nuclear war was narrowly avoided

**17th of May Incident (1979) → Time of Troubles (1979-1995):**
- The National Party had dominated politics since the 1900s and supported the Ustayaris System, militarism, and colonial rule
- Political violence increased due to growing resistance against the colonial system established in the 17th century
- Opposition came in great part from disillusioned veterans, younger generations, segments of the middle and upper classes and colonial populations
- Military influence grew substantially during the Gyattislander Bush War due to the persistent insurgency
- When protests reached their peak, the Emergency Management Council (EMC) overthrew the civilian government of the National Party
- Under the EMC, many pre-existing racial laws were repealed, the National Party was banned and Bantustans were abolished
- However, opposition politicians and journalists were persecuted, and former officials, including former Prime Minister Osman Reis (a hardliner who was the final National Party prime minister before the 17th of May Incident), were executed
- Civilian government was eventually restored in 1982
- Critics argued that the transition represented a military dictatorship voluntarily giving up power rather than a true democratic revolution
- The centre-left Republican Union won the first elections after the transition on a platform of stamping out terrorism and restoring Cartegenia’s honour
- The final racial laws were repealed in 1995 after years of instability and a prolonged loyalist insurgency, ending the Ustayaris System and colonial-era inequality once and for all

**Third Skiyian Crisis (1982):**
- The Taunfurt Pact conducted one of its largest military exercises in history: Militarübung 82
- The exercise was so realistic that FNA intelligence interpreted it as a possible preparation for an actual conventional war
- The world once again approached a major crisis
- Rapusian clarification of the exercise’s defensive nature helped calm tensions in the FNA

## THEMES

- Satire of ideological extremism, black-and-white thinking, motivated reasoning, dehumanisation, and hypocrisy; all of which are treated with complete internal seriousness within the world. The satire is ideally legible to readers; but Nova Terrans simply experience it as their world (down to complaining about it).
- Good guys sometimes lose. "Good guy" is subjective.
- Genuine incompetence and genuine accidents coexist with deliberate calculation. Not everything is a conspiracy.
- Nova Terra as a setting also regularly parodies the tropes of bad worldbuilding by either taking them far more seriously than what would be expected or by subverting them.
- **Design philosophy:** Most nations began as fundamentally silly concepts and were elaborated through serious internal logic until they became genuinely complex while retaining the original characteristic. The silliness is generative, not limiting.
